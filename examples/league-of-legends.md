## What's changed

**The patch right now**
- You may believe the live patch is around 26.19–26.20 and that Worlds runs on a patch locked in mid-September. As of 2026-10-06:
  - **26.19 has been live since Wed 2026-09-23.** **26.20 goes live Wed 2026-10-07**, and Riot calls it **the Worlds patch**. The 26.19 notes say "the Worlds patch (which is the patch *after* this one)".
  - The next patches are 26.21 (Oct 21, mid-Worlds), 26.22 (Nov 4), 26.23 (Nov 18) and 26.24 (Dec 9).
  - The client and Data Dragon call it **16.19.1**. Pro stat sites show 16.x too: 16.17 = 26.17.
  - ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/), [patch schedule](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/patch-schedule-league-of-legends/), [Data Dragon](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/api/versions.json), 2026-10)
- You may believe hotfix "B-patches" are routine. Riot now appends dated **Mid-Patch Updates** to the notes, and they are rare: only 26.1, 26.3, 26.6, 26.9 and 26.10 (a one-day-late Smolder nerf) had one in 2026. ([26.9 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/), 2026)
- **26.19 balance changes.** Riot called this the "first round of pro-focused" changes.
  - **Buffs:** Aatrox, Aphelios, Aurora, Draven, Elise, Fiora, Kha'Zix, Lillia (R sleep 2→2.5s) and Volibear.
  - **Lucian:** reshaped to depend less on enchanters.
  - **Nerfs:** Nasus, Nocturne (R cooldown 160/130/100), Poppy, Rumble, Ryze and Vi (passive shield 12→10% max health).
  - **Master Yi:** his Q refund is now a flat 1s.
  - **Support starter items:** World Atlas and Runic Compass trade health for health regen.
  - **Top role quest:** Teleport cooldown 420→390s; Unleashed Teleport 300–210s.
  - **Team Voice:** planned for NA/OCE in 26.20, now **postponed** with no new date.
  - ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/), 2026-09)
- **26.20**, previewed by Phroxzon on Sep 29–30 and confirmed by the final notes on Oct 6. Phroxzon called it the "final Worlds patch".
  - **Nerfs:** Ambessa, Cassiopeia and K'Sante ("stale in pro play"); Ashe (AD growth 3.5→3); Yunara (level-1 laning).
  - **Item nerfs:** Experimental Hexplate for ranged users; Runaan's bolt 65→60% AD; Hextech Rocketbelt (ability haste 20→10, bigger active).
  - **Buffs:** Lucian, Vayne (flat 30 mana Q), Diana, Lillia (R sleep 2.5/2.75/3s), Kennen, Mordekaiser, Neeko, Smolder, Swain and Tahm Kench.
  - **Kindred:** buffed (W monster damage up, E slow longer, E no longer targets small monsters).
  - **Pulled from the patch:** Varus and Sylas changes, though some outlets still list them.
  - ([Phroxzon preview](https://x.com/RiotPhroxzon/status/2104800631001415990), numbers via [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-20-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/), 2026-09-30)

**2026 season structure and systems**
- You may believe 2026 Season 1 was Demacia-themed and that the year has three themed seasons. True, with details:
  - **S1 "For Demacia"** started at 26.1 (Jan 8). It reskinned the map and added the Demacia Rising PvE metagame.
  - **S2 "Pandemonium"** started at 26.9 (Apr 29) and lasted only 6 patches.
  - **S3 "Classic"** started at 26.15 (Jul 29) and runs to Jan 5, 2027. Act II, "Riot Records", starts Oct 7 ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Classic_(Season_2026))).
  - Each season has its own rewards, but **ranks reset only once a year, in January** ([Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/guide/lol-season-guide)).
  - The exception: Master and above in NA, EUW, EUNE, BR, LAN and TR were hard-reset at 26.9.
  - ([2026 annual cycle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Patch/2026_Annual_Cycle), [apex reset /dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-apex-tier-ranked-reset/), 2026)
- You may believe Feats of Strength and Atakhan are in the game, with Baron at 25:00. **Patch 26.1 (Jan 8) removed Feats of Strength, Atakhan and Blood Roses.** Objectives now:
  - **Baron** spawns at **20:00** again and scales with champion level.
  - **First Blood** gives +100 gold; the **first turret** gives a 300-gold bonus shared among nearby allies.
  - **Voidgrubs** spawn once at 8:00 and despawn at 14:45. **Herald** spawns at 15:00 and despawns at 19:45 ([Voidgrub](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Voidgrub), [Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald)).
  - **Drakes:** first at 5:00, each kill worth 75 gold (was 25), with stronger Dragon Vengeance. All epic monsters scale with champion level, not game time.
  - ([26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor), 2026-01)
- **Turrets and pace (26.1).**
  - Plates no longer fall at 14:00. They are permanent, sit on outer, inner and inhibitor turrets, and lose 10 gold of value per minute from 11:00 (capped at −40).
  - Melee champions deal +20% damage to turrets.
  - **Crystalline Overgrowth:** turrets left standing grow crystals, and the next hit on them deals bonus damage.
  - Nexus turrets respawn at 40% health.
  - Minions spawn at **0:30** (was 1:05), jungle camps at 0:55 and Scuttle at 2:55.
  - Base crit damage is **200%** (was 175%).
  - Lane-swap detection was deleted.
  - ([26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), 2026-01)
- **Role quests now exist for every role** (26.1). They draft-lock lanes, because each player's lane is visible in champion select.
  - **Top** (1,200 points): free Teleport, upgraded later to Unleashed Teleport; level cap 20; bonus XP.
  - **Mid** (1,350): free tier-3 boots, and since 26.9 +6% bonus AD/AP (8% since 26.11). This replaced the original fast recall.
  - **Bot** (1,350): +300 gold, extra gold per minion and takedown, and boots move to a 7th "quest" slot.
  - **Jungle:** pet treats, then gold, XP and movement speed.
  - **Support** (800): the World Atlas chain to the same five upgrades. Passive gold is 9 per 10s. Control wards sit in the quest slot. Vigilant Wardstone is gone. Supports lose 33% of minion XP and gold away from bot lane until level 5 (26.16), to stop roaming "second junglers".
  - ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest), [26.16 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/), 2026)
- **Faelights** (26.1): placing a ward on a marked spot gives +25% vision radius and reveals a bonus area for 45s. 26.3 nerfed the river reveals. ([26.3 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-3-notes/), 2026-02)

- **Ranked changes (2026).**
  - **Autofill:** autofilled players are matched against each other by position, and **Aegis of Valor** gives them protection. Since 26.15, Aegis means double LP on wins only, with no loss protection ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/)).
  - **Fairness:** a climb indicator flags players whose MMR is above their rank; victims of AFK or trolling players get full LP refunds; games can be ended early by vote when griefing is detected (26.10) ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/), [u.gg](https://u.gg/lol/news/patch-26-10-lets-league-of-legends-players-end-ruined-games-early)).
  - **Dodging and bans:** a Master+ dodge counts as a loss, and a ban applies to all of a person's accounts ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/), [penalties FAQ](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/account-penalties-and-enforcement-faq/)).
  - **Duo:** since 26.15, Master players can duo only with others close in rank, and **Grandmaster and Challenger cannot duo**.
  - **WASD movement** is allowed in Ranked (from shortly after 26.9) ([26.9 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/)).
  - ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/), [apex /dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-apex-tier-ranked-reset/), 2026)

  - **League Classic** (26.15): an unranked throwback to the 2013-era game, with 60 champions on their classic kits, runes and masteries, and IP. Its roster grows through community "Council" votes ([/dev: Classic](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-league-of-legends-classic/)).
  - **Swiftplay** was overhauled in 26.1 ([26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)).

## What an expert knows
- **How the 2026 game plays.** Removing Atakhan and Feats cut forced early teamfights. Permanent but decaying plates, the melee turret bonus, Crystalline Overgrowth and a 0:30 first wave reward laning, split-pushing and sieging. Baron at 20:00 is again the mid-game pivot. Lanes are fixed by role quests, so lane swaps and support "second jungler" roams are dead or penalised. Top's quest Teleport freed top laners from taking Teleport, and Riot says top lane loses influence at higher tiers (a paraphrase, not a quote).
- **Pro and solo queue now pull apart sharply.**
  - Cassiopeia, K'Sante, Orianna and Ryze are pro priorities but below 50% in solo queue. Yone, Malphite, Jinx and Thresh dominate solo queue.
  - Riot openly nerfs "pro skew" picks and protects solo queue, so late-season patches (26.18–26.20) are tuned around Worlds.
  - Korean Master+ solo queue (Lee Sin 56% ban rate, high Yunara, Jayce and Viktor) looks more like pro play than global Emerald+.
- **Reading stats sites.**
  - Compare a win rate with its bracket's average. On lolalytics, Emerald+ averages about **51.7%**, not 50%; Jinx shows 54.4% there and 52.5% on op.gg.
  - High win rates at 1–2% pick rate (mage or Yasuo bot) come from small, self-selected samples, not meta.
  - Real meta shifts build up over several patches through item changes. Yunara's rise followed the 26.16 Runaan's buff, which 26.20 partly reverts.
- **How Riot communicates.**
  - Phroxzon (Matt Leung-Harrison, Lead Gameplay Designer: champions, balance, preseason and modes) posts a preview on X about 8 days before each patch.
  - The notes land at about 18:00 UTC the day before the patch.
  - Dev Update videos come with written TL;DW recaps.
  - The public balance-threshold framework (Average, Skilled, Elite and Pro groups) dates from 2020 and has no published 2026 update.
- **Pro calendar habits.** Pro leagues lag about one patch behind solo queue: the 2026 playoffs ran on 26.16–26.17 while 26.18 and 26.19 were live. Worlds itself starts on 26.20, and **26.21 lands mid-tournament**. Ask which patch a stat comes from.
- **Live debates.**
  - **Role quests as "homework"** (Chinese community, Hupu) vs. Riot's "rock-paper-scissors" strategy pitch. Riot's /dev framed 2026 to Korean players as strategy-first. Verdict: quests made lanes more decisive; the jungle feels sidelined (Kanavi: "Jungle is definitely pretty bad").
  - **Non-ADC bot in pro.** Korean press calls it the keyword of 2026; fans want hypercarry duels back.
  - **Monetization.**
    - The Season 2 pass cut skin choices and was walked back.
    - A Brazilian court fined Riot R$15M over loot boxes; the ruling is not final.
    - Caps' Hall of Legends bundle costs 58,865 RP.
  - **Esports contraction:** the LTA failed, the LCS returned with 8 teams, and the LPL shrank from 16 to 14 teams.
- **Vanguard.**
  - An optional On-Demand mode needs Windows 11 25H2 with Secure Boot, TPM and VBS; about 35% of players are eligible.
  - 296k accounts across LoL and VALORANT combined were actioned for rank manipulation (2026-09).
  - The "under 1% of games have a scripter" figure dates from 2024.
