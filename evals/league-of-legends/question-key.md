## Q1: TRAP

**Question:** Norra "got added in 26.18" and keeps beating the user in mid lane on Syndra. What are her power spikes, and how should the user lane against her?

### Why it is a trap

**Norra is not a champion in League of Legends on PC.** She was not added in 26.18 or in any other PC patch.

Norra (the yordle "Portal Mistress", Yuumi's owner in the lore) is a real Riot character. She is playable in **Wild Rift**, the separate mobile game, where she is its first "first-to-the-Rift" champion and launched on 2026-02-04. The PC game's only new champion of 2026 is **Locke**, released in patch 26.13 on 2026-06-24. Patch 26.18 added no new champion. Its headline content was:

- Caps' Hall of Legends induction.
- Five legacy Demacian champions added to League Classic: Fiora, Galio, Poppy, Shyvana and Xin Zhao.
- Balance changes.

The premise is plausible because Norra is a real champion in another Riot game, but it is false for PC LoL.

### Evidence

- **Wild Rift Patch Notes 7.0b (2026-02-03).** "Norra will be released on February 4 at 00:01 UTC." The notes call her "our first to the Rift champion, Norra". https://wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/wild-rift-patch-notes-7-0b/
- **Wild Rift dev article "Norra: The Long Way Home" (2026-02-07).** "Norra is an artillery mage in Wild Rift, built to deal devastating magic damage from long range." Also: "For our first first-to-the-Rift champion, it was important for us to design her kit to be mobile first." https://wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/norra-the-long-way-home/
- **Norra's champion page exists only on the Wild Rift site.** https://wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/norra/
- **The official PC champion list (checked 2026-10-06) has no Norra.**
  - Champions starting with N: Naafiri, Nami, Nasus, Nautilus, Neeko, Nidalee, Nilah, Nocturne, Nunu & Willump.
  - Locke and Zaahen are listed.
  - https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/
- **PC patch 26.18 notes (2026-09-09) contain no new champion release.** https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes/
- **PC patch 26.13 notes (2026-06-23):** Locke releases June 24. https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-13-notes/
- **PCGamesN (updated 2026-06-17):** "Locke, an AP mid assassin. He is champion 173." Also: "He is the only champion releasing this year." https://www.pcgamesn.com/league-of-legends/new-champion
- **op.gg (patch 16.19, checked 2026-10-06) has no PC champion page for Norra.** The URL falls back to the tier list. https://op.gg/lol/champions/norra/build
- **Background, not required:** Riot has said Norra stays on Wild Rift for now. Rumors list her as a possible 2027 PC champion, but nothing is official. Escorenews and Sheepesports coverage, early 2026.

### Expected behaviour

**A good answer:**

- Clearly says Norra is not playable on PC League of Legends and was not added in 26.18.
- Explains that she is a Wild Rift-exclusive champion (first-to-the-Rift, launched 2026-02-04). Optionally adds that she appears in lore, TFT and Legends of Runeterra.
- Asks whether the user means Wild Rift or a different champion. A natural guess is **Locke**, PC's new 2026 mid-lane AP assassin, or another mage.

If the answer then gives Wild Rift-specific advice and explicitly labels it as Wild Rift, that is acceptable.

**Fail:**

- Treating Norra as a PC champion.
- Inventing a PC kit, power spikes, patch history or matchup advice for her as if she were on Summoner's Rift on PC.

## Q2: CURRENT FACT (mid lane role quest as it is today)

**Question:** What does finishing the mid lane role quest give right now, and what's the fastest way to finish it?

### Key facts a correct, current answer should contain

**Reward right now:**

- **Free Tier 3 boots.** Your Tier 2 boots upgrade to an exclusive Tier 3 version (boots bought after completion upgrade on purchase):

  | Tier 2 boots | Tier 3 version |
  |---|---|
  | Sorcerer's Shoes | Spellslinger's Shoes |
  | Ionian Boots | Crimson Lucidity |
  | Berserker's Greaves | Gunmetal Greaves |
  | Boots of Swiftness | Swiftmarch |
  | Mercury's Treads | Chainlaced Crushers |
  | Plated Steelcaps | Armored Advance |
  | Gluttonous Greaves | Immortal Path |

  Gluttonous Greaves was added in 26.9.
- **+8% bonus AD and +8% bonus AP.**

Sources:

- LoL Wiki "Role Quests", checked 2026-10-06: https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quests
- Patch 26.11 notes (2026-05-27). "Bonus AD and AP: 6% ⇒ 8%", with the comment "allowing mid laners their own special form of scaling". https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-11-notes/

**Empowered Recall no longer exists.** It was the original 26.1 reward: 300-second cooldown, reduced by 60 seconds per takedown, cutting the recall from 8 to 4 seconds. Patch 26.9 replaced it with +6% bonus AD/AP: "we're replacing the empowered recall effect with a percent-based increase to bonus AD and AP."

- Patch 26.9 notes (2026-04-28): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/
- Patch 26.1 notes (2026-01-07): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/

**Requirement and point sources.** You need 1350 points:

| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Champion takedown | 25 |
| Damage to champions | 3% of damage dealt (melee) or 1.5% (ranged) |
| Minion killed in lane | 2 |
| Turret taken in lane | 50 |
| Plate taken in lane | 40 |
| Epic monster | 30 |
| Passive gain | about 1.5 points per second while "in the quest lane" (anywhere in your lane outside base, since 26.9); about 0.333 per second elsewhere |

Sources: Role Quests wiki (checked 2026-10-06); patch 26.1 notes (2026-01-07); patch 26.9 notes (2026-04-28).

**Things that slow you down or help you.** All from patch 26.9 (2026-04-28):

- CS, turret and plate points earned outside your lane are cut by up to 75%. The penalty shrinks to 0% as quest progress rises.
- Recalling turns off passive point gain for 12 seconds.
- After level 3 you bank roaming time: up to 60 seconds of roaming for every 120 seconds spent in lane.

A good "fastest way" answer therefore covers:

- Staying in lane for the passive gain.
- Last-hitting in lane.
- Trading damage, which matters more for melee champions.
- Taking plates and the turret.
- Avoiding unnecessary or early recalls.
- Using the roam bank instead of roaming all the time.

**Context that makes the reward valuable.** Feats of Strength, which used to grant Tier 3 boots, was removed in 26.1. So the mid quest is the only source of Tier 3 boots. Patch 26.1 notes (2026-01-07).

**Nice to have.** Patch 26.16 (2026-08-11) tuned boots to favour offensive choices in mid:

- Spellslinger's Shoes magic penetration: 18 + 8% → 20 + 8%.
- Chainlaced Crushers magic resistance: 30 → 25.
- Berserker's Greaves attack speed: 25% → 30% (Tier 3: 40% → 45%).

Source: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/

### Red flags (outdated or wrong)

- Says the reward is "Tier 3 boots + Empowered Recall". That was true before 26.9.
- Says "+6% AD/AP". That was true before 26.11.
- Mentions Feats of Strength boots.
- Doesn't know role quests exist.

## Q3: JUDGMENT (top lane: Teleport vs Ignite with role quests)

**Question:** Is Teleport still worth taking in top lane? When should you take TP, and when should you take Ignite or something else?

### Key facts a correct, current answer should rest on

**What the top quest gives on completion.** The quest needs 1200 points. What you get depends on whether you brought Teleport:

| | You did **not** take Teleport | You **did** take Teleport |
|---|---|---|
| Teleport reward | You gain **Unleashed Teleport as a bonus (extra) summoner spell** | Your Teleport is upgraded to Unleashed Teleport |
| Cooldown | 390 seconds (was 420 before 26.19) | 300–210 seconds (was 330–240 before 26.19) |
| Shield | None | On arrival, a shield worth **35% of max health for 10 seconds** (was 30% for 30 seconds before 26.12) |

You also get these in both cases:

- 600 XP.
- +11% XP from non-champion sources.
- +80 XP per champion takedown. This replaced a flat +12.5% XP in 26.9.
- Level cap raised to 20.

Sources:

- Patch 26.19 notes (2026-09-22), cooldown lines and rationale: "it's become clear that top lane is slightly weak, especially for split pushing." https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/
- Patch 26.12 notes (2026-06-09): "Max Health Shield: 30% for 30 seconds ⇒ 35% for 10 seconds". The same section says "In a post-rolequest world… everyone gets teleport!" https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-12-notes/
- Patch 26.9 notes (2026-04-28), XP change: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/
- Role Quests wiki, checked 2026-10-06: https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quests

**Current play patterns** (op.gg, patch 16.19 = 26.19, Emerald+, checked 2026-10-06):

|---|---|---|
| Fiora | 85.7% | 8.9% |

| Jax | 67.0% | 23.4% |
| Shen | 65.7% | 30.6% |
| Ornn | 19.4% | **76.2%** |

Ornn is the exception: tanks still mostly take TP. Lolalytics (16.19, Emerald+) also shows Flash + Ignite as Aatrox's main spell pair.

Source pages:

- https://op.gg/lol/champions/fiora/build/top
- https://op.gg/lol/champions/aatrox/build/top
- https://op.gg/lol/champions/jax/build/top

### Judgment a good answer should reach

Because every top laner gets a free Unleashed Teleport once the quest is done, a combat summoner is now the default for lane bullies, duelists and all-in champions. That means Ignite, or Ghost or Exhaust depending on the champion.

Teleport is still worth taking:

- For tanks, scaling champions, or champions who lose lane.
- When you need early map presence or safe returns before the quest completes. Completion is a mid-game event: roughly 13+ minutes from passive gain alone, sooner with CS and plates (approximate).
- When you want the stronger upgraded TP: shorter cooldown plus the 35% shield, for cross-map plays or split pushing.

The answer should frame this as depending on the champion and matchup. It should mention that 26.19 just made both quest-TP versions better.

### Red flags

- "Always take TP, it's standard," with no mention of the free quest TP.
- Presenting outdated numbers as current: a 7-minute or 420-second cooldown, a 30% shield lasting 30 seconds, or +12.5% XP.
- Saying TP becomes Unleashed at 14:00 for everyone, as in 2025.
- No awareness of role quests.

## Q6: CURRENT FACT (League Classic)

**Question:** What is League Classic, does anything carry over to the normal account or rank, and which champions are playable right now?

### Key facts

**What it is:**

- A **permanent** nostalgia mode. It launched on **2026-07-29** with patch 26.15, at the start of Season 3.
- Riot calls it "more of a collection of greatest hits" of early League, anchored on **Season 3 (2013)**. It has:
  - Pre-rework champion kits.
  - Old items such as Heart of Gold, Deathfire Grasp and Atma's/Warmog's builds.
  - Old **Runes** (marks, seals, glyphs, quintessences) and **Masteries**.
  - **IP** currency.
  - Free "Classic Skins".

Sources:

- Patch 26.15 notes (2026-07-28): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/
- /dev: League of Legends Classic (2026-07-14): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-league-of-legends-classic/
- TechTimes (2026-07-29): https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322043/20260729/league-legends-classic-live-season-3-kits-60-champions-no-ranked.htm

**Queues and ranked:**

- At launch there was a PvP draft queue, Co-op vs AI and custom games.
- **There is no ranked.** Riot's goal is "a place for you to have fun with your friends, rather than a sweaty Ranked grindfest."
- Sources: /dev article (2026-07-14); TechTimes (2026-07-29).

**What carries over:**

- Classic has **separate progression**: Classic Levels, IP, Classic rune pages and masteries, and its own Summoner's Journey.
- It does not touch your main-game rank or MMR.
- Champions you **already own** are available right away. Others unlock through your Classic Level or the Shop.
- Classic Levels can grant rewards including Blue Essence (BE) and cosmetics.
- Sources: /dev (2026-07-14) says "If you already own a champion, you'll have access to them in Classic right away" and that Classic Levels unlock "Masteries, Runes, the Summoner's Journey, IP, BE, as well as a variety of cosmetics". TechTimes (2026-07-29) says rank and MMR are unaffected and calls it a "fully separate progression database".

**Roster right now: 72 champions.**

- At launch: 60, which Riot describes as "the OG 40 plus another 20 hand-selected champions released from 2009-2013".
- "No Yasuos allowed."
- Added since launch:
  - 26.16: Akali, Kennen, Shen.
  - 26.18: Fiora, Galio, Poppy, Shyvana, Xin Zhao.
  - 26.19: Fizz, Graves, Nami, Nautilus (the "water-themed" drop).
- The official Classic champions page lists 72, checked 2026-10-06. The list:
  - Ahri, Akali, Alistar, Amumu, Anivia, Annie, Ashe, Blitzcrank, Brand, Cho'Gath.
  - Corki, Dr. Mundo, Evelynn, Ezreal, Fiddlesticks, Fiora, Fizz, Galio, Gangplank, Garen.
  - Gragas, Graves, Heimerdinger, Janna, Jarvan IV, Jax, Karthus, Kassadin, Katarina, Kayle.
  - Kennen, Kog'Maw, Lee Sin, Leona, Lulu, Lux, Malphite, Malzahar, Master Yi, Miss Fortune.

## Q7: RECENT CHANGE (Sundered Sky nerfs and what fighters build now)

**Question:** Sundered Sky bruisers (Vi, Aatrox) feel much weaker than a couple of months ago. What did Riot change on Sundered Sky, and what do fighters build now?

### Key facts

**Patch 26.16 (2026-08-11): first nerf.** Riot wrote: "Sundered Sky… has been the meta dominant AD fighter item for the last year… it's healing for more than even lifesteal based items." The changes:

| Stat | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Healing | 100% (melee) / 50% (ranged) of base AD + 6% of missing health | 90% / 45% of base AD + 4% of missing health |
| Health | 400 | 450 (compensation) |
| Recipe | Tunneler + Caulfield's + Ruby Crystal + 500g | Tunneler + Caulfield's + 900g |

The same patch buffed the alternatives:

- **Black Cleaver:** AD 40 → 45; its move speed bonus is now 20 for melee and 10 for ranged users.
- **Sterak's Gage:** bonus AD 45% → 50% of base AD.
- **Eclipse:** damage 6% (melee) / 4% (ranged) → 8% / 5% of max health.
- **Tiamat:** AD 20 → 25.

Source: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/

**Patch 26.17 (2026-08-25): second nerf.** Riot wrote: "Last patch's Sundered Sky nerf was a step in the right direction, but landed a bit under-target." The changes:

- Health: 450 → 400.
- AD: 45 → 40.

The same patch buffed Stormrazor's attack speed (20% → 25%) and several melee carries. Source: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/

**Current Sundered Sky:**

- 40 AD, 10 Ability Haste, 400 health. Costs 3100 gold.
- Passive: the empowered attack crits and heals for 90% (melee) / 45% (ranged) of base AD plus 4% of missing health, with a 10-second cooldown per target.
- Earlier, in 26.1, it had gone from 40 to 45 AD, its crit ratio from 100% to 80%, and its cooldown from 8 to 10 seconds.
- Source: LoL Wiki "Sundered Sky", checked 2026-10-06. https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sundered_Sky

**Follow-up in patch 26.19 (2026-09-22):**

- **Aatrox** was buffed because "The Darkin Blade has lost a fair bit of power with Sundered Sky nerfs". W cooldown went from 20/18/16/14/12 to 18/16.5/15/13.5/12. His E heal went from 1.1% to 1.3% per 100 bonus health.
- **Vi** was nerfed "now that Sundered Sky is in a better spot". Her AD went from 63 + 3.5/level to 61 + 3.9/level, and her passive shield from 12% to 10%.
- The **Kha'Zix** notes say "the jungle is no longer dominated by Sundered Sky users."
- Source: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/

**What fighters build now** (patch 16.19 = 26.19, Emerald+, checked 2026-10-06):

- **Aatrox** usually opens with **Eclipse**, then Sundered Sky or Spear of Shojin, then Death's Dance. Other options are Black Cleaver, Sterak's, Serylda's and Endless Hunger.
- **Jax** builds Trinity Force → Sundered Sky → Zhonya's. Sundered Sky is still common as a second item rather than a rush.
- AP or hybrid fighters can use Dusk and Dawn and Hextech Gunblade.

A good answer conveys that Sundered Sky is no longer the automatic first item, while Eclipse, Black Cleaver and Sterak's have regained ground.

## Q8: JUDGMENT (autofilled to support: dodge or play, and how autofill protection works now)

**Question:** The player keeps getting autofilled to support in ranked. Should they dodge or play it out, and how does autofill protection work now?

### Key facts

**Aegis of Valor today (Season 3, since patch 26.15).** Riot's wording: "it will no longer reduce LP losses in the case of defeat, and wins will award double LP without requiring a minimum mastery score."

- Autofilled win: double LP.
- Autofilled loss: normal LP loss.
- No grade requirement.

Sources:

- Patch 26.15 notes (2026-07-28): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/
- A third-party explainer checked against 26.19 (senseos.gg, 2026-10-02) confirms it: "A loss costs your normal LP, and no mastery grade is needed any more." https://senseos.gg/guides/league-of-legends/how-aegis-of-valor-works/

**How the rules evolved** (useful for spotting outdated answers):

- **26.1:** a mastery grade of C or better gave either no LP loss on a defeat or double LP on a win.
  - /dev: Ranked 2026 (2025-12-01): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/
  - Patch 26.1 notes (2026-01-07).
- **26.3:** the required grade rose from C to B. Patch 26.3 notes (2026-02-03): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-3-notes/
- **26.15:** loss protection and the grade requirement were both removed.

**Dodging does not get you out of autofill.** Riot's wording: "If a player dodges an autofilled game during champ select, the status would carry on until the player plays their autofilled game." Normal dodge penalties still apply, and dodging costs more LP at Master and above. Sources: patch 26.1 notes (2026-01-07); /dev: Ranked 2026 (2025-12-01).

**Autofill matchmaking.**

- Autofilled players are matched against autofilled players in the same position where possible, for example autofill support against autofill support. This has been enabled in all regions since 26.7 (2026-03-31). https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-7-notes/
- Aegis rewards are voided for players who go AFK or are reported and validated for disruptive behaviour. Patch 26.5 notes (2026-03-03): https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-5-notes/

**Support context for an autofilled player.** This is useful but not required.

- The support quest needs 800 points of World Atlas or Runic Compass gold. On completion the item upgrades to Bounty of Worlds, and Control Wards cost 40 gold in the quest slot.
- Since 26.16, roaming early is penalised: a 33% penalty outside bot lane until level 5. Hail of Blades was also nerfed for melee champions.
- 26.19 moved World Atlas and Runic Compass from flat health to health regeneration, which favours sustained laning for melee supports.
- Sources:
  - Role Quests wiki, checked 2026-10-06.
  - Patch 26.16 notes (2026-08-11).
  - Patch 26.19 notes (2026-09-22).

### Judgment a good answer should reach

Generally, play the game out:

- Dodging costs LP and a queue lockout and does not clear the autofill.
- A win is worth double LP.

Losses are no longer protected, so the player should:

- Pick simple, reliable supports.
- Prioritise safe laning and vision, and play to win rather than for a grade.
- Consider adjusting role preferences to reduce autofill.

### Red flags

- Claims autofilled losses are LP-protected. That stopped being true at the start of Season 3.
- Says a C or B grade is still required.
- Says dodging resets or avoids autofill.
- Is unaware that Aegis of Valor exists.
