**Norra isn't in PC League of Legends.** Patch 26.18 (live Sept 10) added no new champion to Summoner's Rift. The only champions it added went to League Classic: Fiora, Galio, Poppy, Shyvana and Xin Zhao ([26.18 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes/)). The live champion list ([Data Dragon 16.19.1](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.19.1/data/en_US/champion.json)) has no Norra either. The only new PC champion in 2026 is Locke, a male mid assassin released in 26.13.

Norra is **Wild Rift's first exclusive champion**, a mid-lane artillery mage live since Feb 4, 2026 (WR patch 7.0b) ([WR 7.0b notes](https://wildrift.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/wild-rift-patch-notes-7-0b/)). If that's where you're meeting her, here's the Syndra matchup. The numbers come from the [WR wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/WR:Norra). They change between WR patches, and she was nerfed in 7.1f.

**Her kit:**
- **Q:** she channels for up to 2.5s while slowed 20–30%, then fires a bolt with 800 to 1200 range. A full charge does about 40% more damage and hops her 300 units back.
- **E:** two portals close in on you. If they mark you, for 4s your hitbox doubles, you're slowed, and her Q deals extra %max-health damage to you.
- **W:** banishes you for 2.25s with an 80% slow and a silence. Cooldown 23 to 20s.
- **R (unlocks at level 5):** up to 4 long-range volleys that slow.

**Her power spikes:** guides agree she's weak before her first item (Luden's) ([gamingonphone](https://gamingonphone.com/action/wild-rift-norra-build-guide-skill-analysis-best-runes-and-gameplay-tips/), [wildriftcounter](https://wildriftcounter.com/champions/norra/)). She maxes Q first, so her poke keeps growing. At level 5, R gives her skirmish reach, and with Rabadon's she can zone in late teamfights.

**How to lane against her on Syndra:**
1. When she starts charging Q, step in. She's slowed and committed, so land your Q→E stun; it should cancel the channel.
2. Strafe sideways out of the E portals. If you get marked, back off for the 4 seconds.
3. Bait her W before you all-in. It silences your combo, so don't throw Flash or R into it. Once W is down, she has no peel beyond a 300-unit hop.
4. Don't walk over her trinkets. They deal damage and give her vision.
5. Push and trade before her first item, and ping your jungler. She's very fragile and has no real dash.

## Q2
The mid quest takes **1,350 points**.

**What finishing it gives you now:**
- **+8% bonus AD and +8% AP.** This replaced the old reward, an empowered 4-second recall, in 26.9 (at 6%). Patch 26.11 raised it to 8% ([26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/), [26.11](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-11-notes/)). It's a percentage, so it grows with your build and is worth more late.
- **Free Tier 3 boots.** Your Tier 2 boots upgrade at no cost to their exclusive Tier 3 version, for example Berserker's Greaves → Gunmetal Greaves ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)).

**Where the points come from:**

| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Passive, anywhere in mid lane outside your base | 1.5/s (90/min) |
| Passive, elsewhere | ~0.33/s |
| Minion kill | 2 in mid, 1 elsewhere |
| Damage to champions | 1.5% of damage dealt if ranged, 3% if melee |
| Champion takedown | 25 |
| Turret plate (mid) | 40 |
| Turret (mid) | 50 |
| Epic monster takedown | 30, anywhere |

Every 2 minutes in lane also banks 1 minute of roaming at no cost to your quest (added in 26.9). Passive points alone finish the quest at about 15:00.

**The fastest way to finish:**
1. **Stay in lane.** Leaving cuts passive points by about 80%. Roam only in short trips that your banked lane time covers.
2. **Last-hit everything.** A wave is worth about 12 points.
3. **Trade constantly.** Damage to champions counts, and melee mids (Yone, Yasuo, Zed) earn double.
4. **Take plates.** They no longer fall off at 14:00; they're permanent since 26.1 and only their gold decays. Each plate is 40 points and the turret is 50.
5. **Join grubs and drakes.** Each epic monster is 30 points, plus 25 per takedown.

A clean lane that trades and takes a couple of plates usually finishes around **11–13 minutes**. That's my estimate, not an official figure. Plan to own Tier 2 boots by then.

Ignite usually wins 1–2 points more often, but that's partly because people take it into lanes they expect to win.

**Why Ignite works: the quest gives you TP anyway.** The top quest takes 1,200 points, about 13.5 minutes on passive points alone and sooner with farm and plates. Finishing it gives you a level cap of 20, +600 XP and +11% XP ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)). It also gives you a Teleport, depending on your spells:
- **If you didn't take TP:** you get a free Unleashed Teleport in the quest slot with a **390s** cooldown (it was 420s before 26.19). That's effectively a third summoner spell.
- **If you did take TP:** your Unleashed TP drops to a **300–210s** cooldown (other roles keep 330–240s), and you get a 35% max-health shield for 10s when you arrive ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)).

So taking TP buys you two things: Teleport before about minute 11–13, and a better TP for the rest of the game.

**Take TP when:**
- You're a tank, a scaling champion, or a blind pick that can't win lane (Malphite or Ornn style).
- You want to join early fights before your quest is done: grubs at 8:00, first drakes, bot-lane dives.
- Your plan is to split-push. Riot buffed both TPs in 26.19 because "top lane is slightly weak, especially for split pushing."
- You're playing organised five-stacks. LCK top laners Doran, Rich and Casting all said they'd keep TP as a fixed pick ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20054/lck-players-react-to-2026-preseason-patch-teleport-still-essential-for-top-lane-jungle-rewards-underwhelming)).

**Take other spells when:**
- **Ignite:** you're a lane bully or duelist with kill pressure at levels 2–6, or you need anti-heal.
- **Ghost:** you're a juggernaut who must stick to targets, like Darius.
- **Exhaust:** you're facing duelists or assassins. On [lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/darius/build/), Darius's best combo is Flash + Exhaust at about 53%.

Patch 26.20 makes no Teleport changes.

## Q6
**What it is.** League Classic launched in patch 26.15 (Jul 29, 2026) and recreates League from about 2009–2013 ([Riot /dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-league-of-legends-classic/)):
- old-era visuals and pre-rework champion kits;
- classic items like Atma's Impaler and Frozen Mallet, and every old summoner spell;
- the old runes and masteries;
- IP as its currency.

There's one PvP draft queue, plus Co-op vs AI and custom games. It isn't ranked. Riot patches it alongside the main game, but hasn't clearly said whether the mode is permanent.

**What carries over to your normal account:**
- **Rank: no.** Your Solo/Duo or Flex LP and MMR are untouched. Classic has its own Classic Level (up to 30) and its own ladder, Summoner's Journey, which unlocks at Classic Level 10 ([Riot support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/league-classic-progression)).
- **Champions: one way.** Champions you already own are playable in Classic. Others unlock through Classic Level, the Classic shop or free rotation. Riot hasn't said whether champions unlocked in Classic carry back to your main account, so don't count on it.
- **Rewards: some.** The Classic Level track pays out Blue Essence, icons and emotes. Classic skins and portraits work only in Classic, and IP, runes and masteries stay there.

**Who you can play right now (26.19): 72 champions.**
- **The 60 at launch** (per the [Hotspawn guide](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/league-of-legends-classic-guide)): Ahri, Alistar, Amumu, Anivia, Annie, Ashe, Blitzcrank, Brand, Cho'Gath, Corki, Dr. Mundo, Evelynn, Ezreal, Fiddlesticks, Gangplank, Garen, Gragas, Heimerdinger, Janna, Jarvan IV, Jax, Karthus, Kassadin, Katarina, Kayle, Kog'Maw, Lee Sin, Leona, Lulu, Lux, Malphite, Malzahar, Master Yi, Miss Fortune, Morgana, Nasus, Nidalee, Nunu, Olaf, Pantheon, Rammus, Ryze, Shaco, Singed, Sion, Sivir, Skarner, Sona, Soraka, Taric, Teemo, Tristana, Tryndamere, Twisted Fate, Twitch, Vayne, Veigar, Warwick, Wukong, Zilean.

## Q7
**What Riot changed on Sundered Sky**

[26.16](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/) (Aug 12). Riot called it "the meta dominant AD fighter item for the last year". The changes:
- **Heal:** 100% base AD + 6% missing health ⇒ 90% base AD + 4% missing health. For ranged champions, the base-AD part went 50% ⇒ 45%.
- **Recipe:** the Ruby Crystal was removed, so it's now Tunneler + Caulfield's + 900g. Total cost is unchanged, but there's no cheap health component on the way.
- **Health:** 400 ⇒ 450, as compensation.

[26.17](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/) (Aug 26). Riot said the first nerf "landed a bit under-target", so it cut more:
- **Health:** 450 ⇒ 400.
- **Attack damage:** 45 ⇒ 40.

Overall: smaller heals, less AD, and no health component while you build it.

**Vi and Aatrox were then treated differently** ([26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)):
- **Vi was nerfed directly.** Base AD went 63+3.5/level ⇒ 61+3.9/level, and her passive shield went 12% ⇒ 10% of max health. Riot said "now that Sundered Sky is in a better spot". She sits around 48% in Emerald+.
- **Aatrox was buffed.** His W cooldown was cut, and his E heals more per point of bonus health. Riot said the Sundered Sky nerfs "were aimed more at opening up the jungle meta".

**What fighters build now.** 26.16 also buffed the alternatives:
- Eclipse: 6% ⇒ 8% max-health damage for melee.
- Black Cleaver: 40 ⇒ 45 AD.
- Sterak's: 45% ⇒ 50% of base AD.
- Tiamat: 20 ⇒ 25 AD.

The most common builds on op.gg (Emerald+, 26.19):

| Champion | Most common core | Sundered Sky now |
|---|---|---|
| [Aatrox](https://op.gg/lol/champions/aatrox/build/top) | Eclipse → Sundered Sky → Death's Dance | Second item, or replaced by Shojin |
| [Lee Sin](https://op.gg/lol/champions/leesin/build/jungle) | Eclipse → Black Cleaver → Death's Dance | Second item in many builds |

## Q8
**Play them out. Dodging barely helps you now.**

**How autofill protection works** (since [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)):
- **Aegis of Valor:** an autofilled **win gives double LP**, whatever your grade.
- **An autofilled loss costs normal LP.** Riot removed the old loss protection and the C-grade requirement because players were "play[ing] for the grade, not necessarily for the win."
- **Autofill parity:** matchmaking tries to put you against an autofilled player in the same role. If it can't, it balances the number of autofilled players on each team, or gives the team with more of them slightly higher-MMR teammates.
- **Support and jungle mains** also get Aegis at a similar rate, without a notice ([senseos](https://senseos.gg/guides/league-of-legends/how-aegis-of-valor-works/), [altchar](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/league-of-legends-ranked-2026-changes-explained-autofill-updates-aegis-of-valor-dodging-rules-and-faster-queue-times-asZ9b4U04yVM)).

**Why dodging doesn't work:**
- Your **autofill status now survives a dodge**, so you'll probably be autofilled again.
- You still pay for it. The first dodge costs −5 LP and a 6-minute lockout. A second within 24 hours costs −15 LP and 30 minutes ([loltheory](https://blog.loltheory.gg/how-to-dodge-league-of-legends/)). In Master+, a dodge counts as a loss.

**The math:** with roughly equal LP gains and losses, double LP on wins means you break even at about a **33% win rate**. At 45% you average about +7 LP a game, against −5 for dodging.

**How to win more of these games:**
- **Keep 2–3 easy supports ready.** On 26.19 (op.gg Emerald+), Leona (51.9%), Braum (52.3%) and Thresh (52.0%) are strong engage picks, and Janna (51.3%) is a forgiving enchanter ([op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions?position=support&tier=emerald_plus)).
- **Build the quest item.** World Atlas upgrades into Bounty of Worlds, and your control wards go in the quest slot.
- **Don't farm or roam for minions before level 5.** Since 26.16, supports lose 33% of minion gold and XP away from bot lane until then.
- **Expect less early all-in power.** Patch 26.19 swapped flat health for health regen on World Atlas and Runic Compass. That makes melee supports weaker in early all-ins and stronger in drawn-out lanes.
