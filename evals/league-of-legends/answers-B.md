Its only "new" champions were five League Classic additions: Fiora, Galio, Poppy, Shyvana and Xin Zhao ([26.18 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes/)). Norra also isn't in the current client's champion data ([Data Dragon](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.19.1/data/en_US/champion.json)).

Norra (Yuumi's owner) is a **Wild Rift** champion. She's an artillery mage for mid lane and was released there on Feb 4, 2026 as Wild Rift's first exclusive champion. She hasn't come to PC ([Norra, WR wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/WR:Norra)).

The only new PC champion of 2026 is **Locke** (26.13, June 24), a melee AP assassin for mid with about a 30% ban rate in Emerald+ ([op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions)). If he's the one beating you, here's the matchup ([Locke wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Locke)).

**His kit and power spikes**
- **Q:** a 950-range nail skillshot he can recast twice within 4 s. Each hit adds a stack (max 3), and 2+ stacks slow you harder. His autos and his E pop the stacks for bonus damage.
- **E:** a 375-range blink, then an empowered auto that dashes in from 275 extra range. The 10 s cooldown **resets on takedowns**.
- **W:** attack speed and move speed, then a recast that heals him. The recast works even while he's CC'd.
- **R (level 6):** 950 range, 400 radius. It executes anyone inside who drops below 10–12% max HP (+0.5% for each soul he has sealed). Each collected soul also refunds 20% of R's cooldown.
- **Spikes:** level 3, level 6, his first item, and every kill. He runs Electrocute and builds Lich Bane → Shadowflame → Zhonya's.

**Playing it on Syndra**
- **Poke his last-hits.** He's melee and you outrange him, so trade whenever he walks up for CS, especially while his E is down.
- **Dodge Q.** Sidestep the nails. If you're carrying 2 stacks, back off: that's his all-in setup.
- **Stun after the blink.** Hold Scatter the Weak until he blinks in. He can't take the dash while stunned, so the combo breaks.
- **Leave the R zone.** After 6, walk out of the circle instead of trading inside it. Save Flash or an early Zhonya's for it.
- **Shove and ward.** Every kill gives him an E reset and a permanent R stack, so track his roams.

Locke is a real counter. Syndra wins about **48%** against him (Emerald+, 26.19, ~6,750 games), about 2 points below expected ([lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/syndra/vs/locke/build/)). Syndra's W was also nerfed in 26.18, so banning Locke is reasonable.

If you actually meant Wild Rift, that game has its own patches and the advice is different; say so and I'll cover Norra there.
## Q2
On patch 26.19 the mid quest takes **1,350 points** ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)).

**What finishing it gives you**
- **+8% bonus AD and +8% AP.** It was 6% when this was added in 26.9 and went up to 8% in 26.11. The Empowered Recall you may remember from the start of the season was removed in 26.9.
- **Free tier-3 boots.** Your tier-2 boots upgrade at no cost:
  - Sorcerer's → Spellslinger's Shoes (now 20 flat + 8% magic pen, 45 move speed)
  - Ionian → Crimson Lucidity
  - Berserker's → Gunmetal Greaves
  - Swiftness → Swiftmarch
  - Mercury's → Chainlaced Crushers
  - Steelcaps → Armored Advance
  - Gluttonous → Immortal Path

Boots already in your inventory when the quest completes upgrade automatically ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Spellslinger%27s_Shoes)). Have tier-2 boots bought before you finish.

**How you earn points**
- **Passive:** 1.5 per second while in lane, about 90 per minute. This is most of the total.
- **Minions:** 2 each.
- **Takedowns:** 25. That's more than top or bot get (15).
- **Damage to champions:** 1.5% of the damage you deal if you're ranged, 3% if you're melee.
- **Objectives:** 40 per plate, 50 per turret, 30 per epic monster.
- **Penalties:** points earned out of lane are cut by up to 75%, less as your progress goes up. Every 2 minutes in lane banks 1 minute of penalty-free roaming. Passive earning also stops for 12 s after you recall.

**Fastest way to finish**
1. Stay in lane and keep last-hitting, because the passive does most of the work.
2. Trade often. 1,000 damage is 15 points for a ranged mage, and each kill or assist is 25.
3. Hit plates when you have priority. One plate is worth about 27 s of passive.
4. Roam, or join grubs (8:00) and drakes, only with banked grace time.
5. Back less often and only with a reason. Each recall pauses the passive and takes you out of lane.

Passive plus normal CS puts you near 1,250 points by about 12:00. Expect roughly **12–14 minutes in an even lane** and a couple of minutes sooner when you're winning. That's my estimate from the point values, not a published average.
## Q3
Yes, Flash + Ignite is now the solo-queue default, and Riot is fine with that. The 26.19 notes say the top quest "has been quite successful at freeing up top laners to skip Teleport", and that patch buffed the quest Teleport ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)).

**How the quest Teleport works** ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest))
- **Without TP:** at 1,200 points (roughly 11–13 minutes, my estimate) you get a free Teleport in the quest slot (V key). Its cooldown is 390 s, down from 420 in 26.19.
- **Other rewards:** +600 XP, 11% bonus XP and a level cap of 20.
- **With TP:** if you brought Teleport yourself, you don't get a second one. Instead your TP gives a 10 s shield worth 35% of your max HP after each channel. Since 26.19 it also cuts Unleashed TP's cooldown by 30 s, to 300–210 s.

**What top laners actually run** (Emerald+, 26.19, lolalytics)
- [Aatrox](https://lolalytics.com/lol/aatrox/build/) is mostly Flash + Ignite.
- [Ornn](https://lolalytics.com/lol/ornn/build/), a tank, also plays Flash + Ignite most often (54.5% win rate).
- [K'Sante](https://lolalytics.com/lol/ksante/build/) still mostly takes Flash + TP (about 80k games, against 16k on Ignite), though Ignite wins more.
- [Gangplank](https://lolalytics.com/lol/gangplank/build/) is split roughly 50/50.

So bringing TP only gets you a Teleport before minute ~12, plus the shield and cooldown upgrade afterwards. You pay for that with your second spell slot for the whole game.

**Take TP when:**
- You're on the weak side, or playing a scaling pick that will lose lane. TP gets you back to the wave without losing XP or plates, and top's quest rewards XP.
- Your team needs you at early fights: drakes from 5:00, the single grub wave at 8:00, or bot-lane dives.
- You're in coordinated play (premade or Clash), or you're a tank who wants the shield on flank TPs. LCK top laners said in preseason that the upgraded TP was too good to give up ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20054/lck-players-react-to-2026-preseason-patch-teleport-still-essential-for-top-lane-jungle-rewards-underwhelming)).

**Take Ignite when:**
- You can win or punish the lane: Darius, Garen, Riven, Pantheon, Yone and similar.
- You're facing healers such as Aatrox, Fiora, Vladimir or Dr. Mundo.
- You want lane priority. You still get TP later from the quest.

**Other options:**
- **Ghost** for juggernauts that need to stick to targets (Darius, Garen, Nasus, Udyr).
- **Exhaust** against all-in duelists or assassins.

**Objectives and pace**
- **Removed:** Atakhan, Feats of Strength and Blood Roses.
- **Baron:** spawns at **20:00** again.

- **Herald:** up from 15:00 to 19:45.
- **Drakes:** the first spawns at 5:00, and each is now worth 75 gold.
- **Scaling:** all epic monsters scale with champion level, not game time.
- **Bonus gold:** First Blood gives +100 gold. The first turret gives a 300-gold bonus shared among nearby allies.
- **Faster start:** minions spawn at **0:30**, camps at 0:55 and Scuttle at 2:55.

**Turrets**
- **Plates never fall.** They sit on outer, inner and inhibitor turrets. Each plate is worth 10 gold less every minute from 11:00, up to −40.
- **Melee damage:** melee champions deal +20% damage to turrets.
- **Crystalline Overgrowth:** turrets left standing grow crystals, and the next hit deals bonus damage.
- **Nexus turrets** respawn at 40% health.

**Crit** now deals 200% damage (it was 175%).

**Role quests for every role** ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)). Everyone's lane is shown in champion select, and lane swaps are effectively dead.

**Faelights:** warding a marked spot gives your ward a bigger vision radius.

**Items and runes**

## Q6
**What it is.** League Classic launched in patch 26.15 (Jul 29). It recreates League from around Season 3 (2013), with a few pieces from as late as 2015 ([/dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-league-of-legends-classic/)).
- **Gameplay:** old champion kits, the original rune and mastery systems (you buy rune pages with IP), slower projectiles and more stuns.
- **Queues:** normal draft, Co-op vs AI and custom games. **There is no ranked queue.**

**What carries over**
- **Rank:** nothing in Classic affects your Summoner's Rift rank or MMR. Classic has its own progression:
  - Classic Levels 1–30, earned once like account levels.
  - A "Summoner's Journey" ladder from Classic Level 10, running Salt → Wood → … → Legend.
  - Seasonal Classic Passes with free and paid tracks. The first ran Jul 29–Sep 21, and unused pass coins expired ([Riot support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/league-classic-pass-and-content)).
- **Champions:** any champion you own on your account is playable in Classic straight away. Ones you don't own unlock through Classic Levels, with IP in the Classic shop, or via the free rotation. Riot doesn't say that champions bought with Classic IP unlock in normal League, so don't count on it.
- **Currency:** IP only exists in Classic. Riot lists Blue Essence among the Classic Level and pass rewards, and that's probably the only reward that reaches your normal account.
- **Skins:** many of your existing skins work in Classic. Classic Skins and Classic Chromas work only in Classic.

**Who you can play: 72 champions as of 26.19** ([official list](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/classic/champions/))

The 60 at launch: Ahri, Alistar, Amumu, Anivia, Annie, Ashe, Blitzcrank, Brand, Cho'Gath, Corki, Dr. Mundo, Evelynn, Ezreal, Fiddlesticks, Gangplank, Garen, Gragas, Heimerdinger, Janna, Jarvan IV, Jax, Karthus, Kassadin, Katarina, Kayle, Kog'Maw, Lee Sin, Leona, Lulu, Lux, Malphite, Malzahar, Master Yi, Miss Fortune, Morgana, Nasus, Nidalee, Nunu & Willump, Olaf, Pantheon, Rammus, Ryze, Shaco, Singed, Sion, Sivir, Skarner, Sona, Soraka, Taric, Teemo, Tristana, Tryndamere, Twisted Fate, Twitch, Vayne, Veigar, Warwick, Wukong, Zilean.

## Q7
Riot nerfed Sundered Sky twice in August ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sundered_Sky)).

**Patch 26.16 (Aug 12)**
- **Healing cut:** from 100% base AD + 6% missing health to **90% base AD + 4% missing health** (melee values).
- **Recipe:** the Ruby Crystal was removed. It's now Tunneler + Caulfield's + 900 gold.
- **Health:** raised from 400 to 450 to compensate.
- **Riot's reason:** it had been "the meta dominant AD fighter item for the last year" and was "healing for more than even lifesteal based items" ([26.16 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)).

**Patch 26.17 (Aug 26)**
- **Compensation reversed:** health went back from 450 to 400, and **AD dropped from 45 to 40**.
- **Riot's reason:** the first nerf "landed a bit under-target" ([26.17 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/)).

**Net change since July**
- 5 less AD.
- The same 400 health.
- About a third less missing-health healing.
- Unchanged: it still costs 3,100 gold, and it still makes one auto per target every 10 s crit and heal.

**Champion changes on top of that** ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/))
- **Vi was nerfed directly in 26.19.** Base AD went from 63 to 61 (growth from 3.5 to 3.9 per level), and her passive shield from 12% to 10% of max health.
- **Aatrox was *buffed* in 26.19.** W cooldown went from 20–12 s to 18–12 s, and E healing from 1.1% to 1.3% per 100 bonus health. He should feel better now than in late August.

**What fighters build now** (Emerald+, 26.19)
- **[Aatrox](https://lolalytics.com/lol/aatrox/build/):** Eclipse → Plated Steelcaps → Spear of Shojin, then Sterak's, Serylda's or Death's Dance. Sundered Sky isn't in his core build.
- **[Lee Sin](https://lolalytics.com/lol/leesin/build/):** Eclipse → Black Cleaver. He builds Sundered Sky only occasionally.
- **[Vi](https://lolalytics.com/lol/vi/build/):** **Trinity Force first**. She keeps Sundered Sky as a 2nd or 3rd item (about 51% win rate), but she's near the bottom of the jungle rankings after 26.19.

The shift happened because 26.16 also buffed the alternatives:
- **Eclipse:** damage against melee targets rose from 6% to 8% of max health.
- **Black Cleaver:** AD rose from 40 to 45.
- **Sterak's Gage:** bonus AD rose from 45% to 50% of base AD.

In practice, ability-based bruisers start Eclipse, auto-attack divers start Trinity Force, and Black Cleaver or Sterak's go second or third. Nothing in the 26.20 preview touches Sundered Sky ([preview](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-20-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/)).
## Q8
**Play these games out.** In 2026, dodging no longer resets autofill: your autofill status carries over to your next game. A dodge costs you the usual LP penalty and queue timer, and you'll most likely be autofilled again. At Master and above, a dodge also counts as a full loss ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/)).

**How autofill works now**
- **Matchmaking:** it first tries to put you against another autofilled player in the same role, so the enemy support is often autofilled too. If it can't, it evens out the number of autofilled players on each team. As a last resort, the team with more autofilled players gets slightly higher-MMR players.
- **Aegis of Valor:** this is the current autofill protection. Since 26.15, when this season started, an autofilled win gives **double LP**, with no mastery-score requirement. A loss costs your normal LP. The old loss protection is gone ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).
- **Keeping it:** champion select tells you when it applies. You lose it if you swap out of your autofilled role, get penalized for disruptive behavior, or the game is remade. Jungle and support queuers also earn it about as often, without being told ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aegis_of_Valor)).
- **No main-role guarantee:** Riot hasn't published a rule that guarantees your main role in the next game.

**The math.** If your LP gains and losses are about the same size, double LP on wins means you break even at about a **33% win rate** (2p = 1 − p). Anything above that is profit.

**Winning as an autofilled support** (Emerald+, 26.19)
- **Picks:** Thresh, Braum, Leona or Blitzcrank are simple and strong. Lulu or Nami if you prefer enchanters.
- **Starting item:** World Atlas. In 26.19 it swapped its flat health for health regen.
- **The quest:** it takes 800 points, which you earn by staying in lane and hitting champions. When it completes, your support item upgrades for free into a final support item and Control Wards cost 40 gold ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)).
- **Don't roam early:** until level 5, minions killed outside bot lane give 33% less gold and XP (26.16), and quest progress outside your lane is penalized.
