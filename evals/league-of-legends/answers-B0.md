## Q2
As of 26.19, finishing the mid quest (1,350 points) gives you two things:
- **A free upgrade to tier-3 boots.**
- **+8% bonus AD/AP.** It arrived at 6% in 26.9 and went up to 8% in 26.11. It applies to the bonus stats from your items, so it gets bigger as you build.

The quest's original reward of a faster Recall is gone; the AD/AP bonus replaced it in 26.9 ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest), [26.9 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/)).

Fastest way to finish it:
- **Farm drives it.** Progress comes mainly from killing minions, plus takedowns. I don't have the exact points for each action, so watch your quest progress in-game, but your CS per minute largely decides when you finish.
  - Don't miss cannons.
  - Don't let waves crash into your tower unattended.
  - Catch a side wave before you back.
- **Takedowns add a chunk.** A roam that gets a kill or assist helps. A roam that costs you a full wave and gets nothing sets you back.
- **Stay in your own lane.** Role quests are built to keep each role in its lane (supports, for example, lose points for leaving bot before level 5). Treat lane farm as your main source.
- **Buy your tier-2 boots first.** Have the ones you'd want upgraded (usually Sorcerer's Shoes on a mage) before the quest completes, so the free tier-3 upgrade becomes stats immediately. I'm not certain how the reward behaves if you finish without tier-2 boots, so don't test it in ranked.

It's worth taking seriously, not treating as homework. Tier-3 boots plus 8% AD/AP is a real power spike. If you finish before the enemy mid, you have a window to force trades or roam with an edge.

## Q3
Mostly no, not as a summoner spell, and that's why you see Flash + Ignite everywhere. The top role quest already gives you Teleport outside your summoner slots. Completing it (1,200 points) upgrades it to Unleashed Teleport.

Patch 26.19 shortened both cooldowns: quest Teleport went from 420s to 390s, and Unleashed Teleport is now 300–210s ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/), [Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)). Riot has said top lane "loses influence at higher tiers", and this change pushes the other way. A summoner Teleport on top would duplicate what you already have, so for almost every top laner that slot is better spent on a combat spell.

So the real choice is what goes next to Flash:
- **Ignite** is the default when you can win or kill in lane: all-in fighters and lane bullies like Darius, Renekton, Riven, Pantheon, Sett, Aatrox and Yone. It also cuts healing against sustain tops like Aatrox, Dr. Mundo, Vladimir, Warwick and Fiora. First Blood is worth +100 gold this year, so early kill pressure pays.
- **Ghost** suits juggernauts that need to reach and stick to targets in fights: Darius, Garen, Olaf, Mordekaiser, Nasus, Volibear, Udyr. With map presence covered by the quest Teleport, Ghost is far easier to justify than it used to be.
- **Exhaust** is for lanes where you mainly need to survive one burst window (into Riven, Fiora, Irelia or Yone), and for tanks or ranged tops who just need to live.
- **Barrier or Cleanse** are niche. Barrier helps squishy ranged tops in kill lanes. Cleanse answers heavy point-and-click crowd control or Ignite.

Use the quest Teleport proactively, especially once it becomes Unleashed. In 2026, plates are permanent, melee champions deal +20% damage to turrets, and Crystalline Overgrowth adds bonus damage to the next hit on a turret that's been left standing. That makes Teleport-backed side-lane pressure the main way top laners shape games now.

I don't have per-champion summoner-spell pick rates for 26.19, so check [op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions) or [lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/tierlist/) for your champion. The 26.20 preview lists no Teleport changes.

## Q6
League Classic is an unranked mode, added in 26.15 (July 29), that recreates the 2013-era game:
- Champions play on their classic kits.
- You use the old runes and masteries, and IP is the currency.
- It launched with 60 champions, and the roster grows through community "Council" votes.
- It's the namesake of Season 3, "Classic", which runs to Jan 5, 2027 ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/), [2026 annual cycle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Patch/2026_Annual_Cycle)).

**What carries over**
- **Rank: nothing.** The mode is unranked, so it can't touch your Solo/Duo or Flex rank or LP.
- **Runes, masteries and IP** are the mode's own throwback systems, not your normal rune pages or Blue Essence.
- I can't confirm whether anything you earn there (IP, unlocks) converts to your main account, or whether it counts toward pass or mission progress. Check the mode's page in the client before you grind it for rewards.

**Which champions you can play**
- It launched with 60, and Council votes have added more since. I don't have the current list, so check the mode's champion select.
- Since it uses classic kits, expect champions from that era on their pre-rework versions. Newer champions like Locke, Yunara or Zaahen won't be there.

Sources disagree on whether League Classic is permanent. If your friend is keen, play it while it's definitely around.

## Q8
Play them out. At this point in the season, the rules favour playing over dodging:
- **Aegis of Valor:** when you're autofilled, a win pays double LP. Since 26.15 there's no loss protection, so a loss costs your normal LP. If your usual gains and losses are about equal, you break even by winning roughly one autofilled game in three. Anything better is profit ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).
- **Fair matchups:** the matchmaker pairs autofilled players against each other by position, so the enemy support is likely off-role too.
- **Protection from bad games:** if a teammate goes AFK or trolls, you get a full LP refund, and games can end early when griefing is detected.
- **Dodging costs you:** you lose LP and get a queue lockout. In Master+, a dodge now counts as a full loss ([apex /dev](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-apex-tier-ranked-reset/)). It also doesn't stop the next autofill.

How to win those support games on 26.19:
- **Champions:** pick simple, strong engage. Leona, Braum and Blitzcrank are all strong this patch. Thresh is the most picked and also strong, but harder to play. Avoid Mel and Karma, both weak in solo queue ([op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions)).
- **Runes and build:** engage supports run Aftershock, with Locket → Knight's Vow → Bandlepipes.
- **Your item is your quest:** buy World Atlas and finish the 800-point chain. As of 26.19, the starter trades some health for health regen. Control wards go in the quest slot.
- **Stay bot until level 5:** before then, supports lose 33% of minion quest points away from bot lane (26.16), so early roams delay your item ([26.16 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)).

I can't confirm whether being autofilled still protects you from being autofilled again the next game; check the Aegis tooltip in champion select. Don't make support your secondary role just to avoid autofill, because then you'd lose the double-LP bonus. Keep two easy supports ready instead.
