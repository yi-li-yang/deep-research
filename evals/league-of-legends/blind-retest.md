**His kit and power spikes**
- **Q:** a 950-range nail skillshot he can recast twice within 4 s. Each hit adds a stack (max 3), and 2+ stacks slow you harder. His autos and his E pop the stacks for bonus damage.
- **E:** a 375-range blink, then an empowered auto that dashes in from 275 extra range. The 10 s cooldown **resets on takedowns**.
- **W:** attack speed and move speed, then a recast that heals him. The recast works even while he's CC'd.
- **R (level 6):** 950 range, 400 radius. It executes anyone inside who drops below 10–12% max HP (+0.5% for each soul he has sealed). Each collected soul also refunds 20% of R's cooldown.
- **Spikes:** level 3, level 6, his first item, and every kill. He runs Electrocute and builds Lich Bane → Shadowflame → Zhonya's.

**Playing it on Syndra**
- **Poke his last-hits.** He's melee and you outrange him, so trade whenever he walks up for CS, especially while his E is down.
- **Dodge Q.** Sidestep the nails. If you're carrying 2 stacks, back off: that's his all-in setup.
- **Stun after the blink.** Hold Scatter the Weak until he blinks in. He can't take the dash while stunned, so the combo breaks.
- **Leave the R zone.** After 6, walk out of the circle instead of trading inside it. Save Flash or an early Zhonya's for it.
- **Shove and ward.** Every kill gives him an E reset and a permanent R stack, so track his roams.

Locke is a real counter. Syndra wins about **48%** against him (Emerald+, 26.19, ~6,750 games), about 2 points below expected ([lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/syndra/vs/locke/build/)). Syndra's W was also nerfed in 26.18, so banning Locke is reasonable.

If you actually meant Wild Rift, that game has its own patches and the advice is different; say so and I'll cover Norra there.

## Q2

**Question:** I main mid and honestly stopped paying attention to the role quest stuff after the season started. What does finishing the mid lane quest actually give me right now, and what's the fastest way to get it done?

### Answer 1

The mid quest takes **1,350 points**.

**What finishing it gives you now:**
- **+8% bonus AD and +8% AP.** This replaced the old reward, an empowered 4-second recall, in 26.9 (at 6%). Patch 26.11 raised it to 8% ([26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/), [26.11](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-11-notes/)). It's a percentage, so it grows with your build and is worth more late.
- **Free Tier 3 boots.** Your Tier 2 boots upgrade at no cost to their exclusive Tier 3 version, for example Berserker's Greaves → Gunmetal Greaves ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)).

**Where the points come from:**

| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Passive, anywhere in mid lane outside your base | 1.5/s (90/min) |
| Passive, elsewhere | ~0.33/s |
| Minion kill | 2 in mid, 1 elsewhere |
| Damage to champions | 1.5% of damage dealt if ranged, 3% if melee |
| Champion takedown | 25 |
| Turret plate (mid) | 40 |
| Turret (mid) | 50 |
| Epic monster takedown | 30, anywhere |

Every 2 minutes in lane also banks 1 minute of roaming at no cost to your quest (added in 26.9). Passive points alone finish the quest at about 15:00.

**The fastest way to finish:**
1. **Stay in lane.** Leaving cuts passive points by about 80%. Roam only in short trips that your banked lane time covers.
2. **Last-hit everything.** A wave is worth about 12 points.
3. **Trade constantly.** Damage to champions counts, and melee mids (Yone, Yasuo, Zed) earn double.
4. **Take plates.** They no longer fall off at 14:00; they're permanent since 26.1 and only their gold decays. Each plate is 40 points and the turret is 50.
5. **Join grubs and drakes.** Each epic monster is 30 points, plus 25 per takedown.

A clean lane that trades and takes a couple of plates usually finishes around **11–13 minutes**. That's my estimate, not an official figure. Plan to own Tier 2 boots by then.

Ignite usually wins 1–2 points more often, but that's partly because people take it into lanes they expect to win.

**Why Ignite works: the quest gives you TP anyway.** The top quest takes 1,200 points, about 13.5 minutes on passive points alone and sooner with farm and plates. Finishing it gives you a level cap of 20, +600 XP and +11% XP ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)). It also gives you a Teleport, depending on your spells:
- **If you didn't take TP:** you get a free Unleashed Teleport in the quest slot with a **390s** cooldown (it was 420s before 26.19). That's effectively a third summoner spell.
- **If you did take TP:** your Unleashed TP drops to a **300–210s** cooldown (other roles keep 330–240s), and you get a 35% max-health shield for 10s when you arrive ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)).

So taking TP buys you two things: Teleport before about minute 11–13, and a better TP for the rest of the game.

**Take TP when:**
- You're a tank, a scaling champion, or a blind pick that can't win lane (Malphite or Ornn style).
- You want to join early fights before your quest is done: grubs at 8:00, first drakes, bot-lane dives.
- Your plan is to split-push. Riot buffed both TPs in 26.19 because "top lane is slightly weak, especially for split pushing."
- You're playing organised five-stacks. LCK top laners Doran, Rich and Casting all said they'd keep TP as a fixed pick ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20054/lck-players-react-to-2026-preseason-patch-teleport-still-essential-for-top-lane-jungle-rewards-underwhelming)).

**Take other spells when:**
- **Ignite:** you're a lane bully or duelist with kill pressure at levels 2–6, or you need anti-heal.
- **Ghost:** you're a juggernaut who must stick to targets, like Darius.
- **Exhaust:** you're facing duelists or assassins. On [lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/darius/build/), Darius's best combo is Flash + Exhaust at about 53%.

Patch 26.20 makes no Teleport changes.

**Net change since July**
- 5 less AD.
- The same 400 health.
- About a third less missing-health healing.
- Unchanged: it still costs 3,100 gold, and it still makes one auto per target every 10 s crit and heal.

**Champion changes on top of that** ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/))

The shift happened because 26.16 also buffed the alternatives:
- **Eclipse:** damage against melee targets rose from 6% to 8% of max health.
- **Black Cleaver:** AD rose from 40 to 45.
- **Sterak's Gage:** bonus AD rose from 45% to 50% of base AD.

In practice, ability-based bruisers start Eclipse, auto-attack divers start Trinity Force, and Black Cleaver or Sterak's go second or third. Nothing in the 26.20 preview touches Sundered Sky ([preview](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-20-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/)).

## Q8

**Question:** I keep getting autofilled to support in ranked. Should I just dodge those lobbies, or is it actually worth playing them out now? How does the autofill protection work at this point in the season?

### Answer 1

**Play them out. Dodging barely helps you now.**

**How autofill protection works** (since [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)):
- **Aegis of Valor:** an autofilled **win gives double LP**, whatever your grade.
- **An autofilled loss costs normal LP.** Riot removed the old loss protection and the C-grade requirement because players were "play[ing] for the grade, not necessarily for the win."
- **Autofill parity:** matchmaking tries to put you against an autofilled player in the same role. If it can't, it balances the number of autofilled players on each team, or gives the team with more of them slightly higher-MMR teammates.
- **Support and jungle mains** also get Aegis at a similar rate, without a notice ([senseos](https://senseos.gg/guides/league-of-legends/how-aegis-of-valor-works/), [altchar](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/league-of-legends-ranked-2026-changes-explained-autofill-updates-aegis-of-valor-dodging-rules-and-faster-queue-times-asZ9b4U04yVM)).

**Why dodging doesn't work:**
- Your **autofill status now survives a dodge**, so you'll probably be autofilled again.
- You still pay for it. The first dodge costs −5 LP and a 6-minute lockout. A second within 24 hours costs −15 LP and 30 minutes ([loltheory](https://blog.loltheory.gg/how-to-dodge-league-of-legends/)). In Master+, a dodge counts as a loss.

**The math:** with roughly equal LP gains and losses, double LP on wins means you break even at about a **33% win rate**. At 45% you average about +7 LP a game, against −5 for dodging.

**How to win more of these games:**
- **Keep 2–3 easy supports ready.** On 26.19 (op.gg Emerald+), Leona (51.9%), Braum (52.3%) and Thresh (52.0%) are strong engage picks, and Janna (51.3%) is a forgiving enchanter ([op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions?position=support&tier=emerald_plus)).
- **Build the quest item.** World Atlas upgrades into Bounty of Worlds, and your control wards go in the quest slot.
- **Don't farm or roam for minions before level 5.** Since 26.16, supports lose 33% of minion gold and XP away from bot lane until then.
- **Expect less early all-in power.** Patch 26.19 swapped flat health for health regen on World Atlas and Runic Compass. That makes melee supports weaker in early all-ins and stronger in drawn-out lanes.

### Answer 2

**Play these games out. Dodging is now strictly worse:**
- **Dodging doesn't get you out of autofill.** Since 26.1, your autofill status carries over to the next lobby ([ranked changes](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/league-of-legends-ranked-2026-changes-explained-autofill-updates-aegis-of-valor-dodging-rules-and-faster-queue-times-asZ9b4U04yVM)).
  - So you pay the dodge penalty and then get autofilled again anyway.
  - Penalty: the first dodge in 24 hours costs 5 LP and a 6-minute lockout. The second costs 15 LP and 30 minutes. After that, 15 LP and 12 hours ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queue_dodging)).
  - In Master and above, dodging an autofilled lobby counts as a full loss.
- **Aegis of Valor (current rules since 26.15).** When the lobby tells you Aegis is active, winning while autofilled gives **double LP**. Losing costs your normal LP ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).
  - Patch 26.15 removed both the old loss protection and the mastery-grade requirement.
  - Aegis only shows up sometimes. It isn't guaranteed every time you're autofilled.
  - You lose it if you swap roles in champion select or get penalized for something like going AFK ([Riot support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/matchmaking-and-autofill)).
- **Matching:** the game tries to put you against an autofilled player in the same role. If it can't, it gives each team the same number of autofilled players. If that fails too, the team with more autofilled players gets slightly higher-MMR teammates.
- **Autofill protection:** after you've been autofilled, you're guaranteed your primary or secondary role "for the next handful of games." Riot doesn't publish the exact number.
- **Priority roles:** players who queue Support or Jungle get Aegis rewards at about the same rate as autofilled players, just without the notice. Choosing **Fill** means you can never be autofilled.

**Practical tips:**
- Keep 2–3 easy supports ready. Strong, low-difficulty picks in Emerald+ on 26.19 ([Lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/tierlist/?lane=support)):

  | Support | Win rate |
  |---|---|
  | Janna | 52.6% |
  | Leona | 53.9% |
  | Sona | 52.9% |
  | Nami | 52.1% |
  | Alistar | 52.6% |

- Don't trade roles in a lobby where Aegis is active.
- If support keeps coming up, try Jungle as your secondary role. You'd still get a role you picked, and Jungle is one of the priority roles that can earn Aegis bonuses.

### Answer 3

**Play these games out.** In 2026, dodging no longer resets autofill: your autofill status carries over to your next game. A dodge costs you the usual LP penalty and queue timer, and you'll most likely be autofilled again. At Master and above, a dodge also counts as a full loss ([/dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026/)).

**How autofill works now**
- **Matchmaking:** it first tries to put you against another autofilled player in the same role, so the enemy support is often autofilled too. If it can't, it evens out the number of autofilled players on each team. As a last resort, the team with more autofilled players gets slightly higher-MMR players.
- **Aegis of Valor:** this is the current autofill protection. Since 26.15, when this season started, an autofilled win gives **double LP**, with no mastery-score requirement. A loss costs your normal LP. The old loss protection is gone ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).
- **Keeping it:** champion select tells you when it applies. You lose it if you swap out of your autofilled role, get penalized for disruptive behavior, or the game is remade. Jungle and support queuers also earn it about as often, without being told ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Aegis_of_Valor)).
- **No main-role guarantee:** Riot hasn't published a rule that guarantees your main role in the next game.

**The math.** If your LP gains and losses are about the same size, double LP on wins means you break even at about a **33% win rate** (2p = 1 − p). Anything above that is profit.

**Winning as an autofilled support** (Emerald+, 26.19)
- **Picks:** Thresh, Braum, Leona or Blitzcrank are simple and strong. Lulu or Nami if you prefer enchanters.
- **Starting item:** World Atlas. In 26.19 it swapped its flat health for health regen.
- **The quest:** it takes 800 points, which you earn by staying in lane and hitting champions. When it completes, your support item upgrades for free into a final support item and Control Wards cost 40 gold ([Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest)).
- **Don't roam early:** until level 5, minions killed outside bot lane give 33% less gold and XP (26.16), and quest progress outside your lane is penalized.
