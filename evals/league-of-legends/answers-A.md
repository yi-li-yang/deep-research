## Q1
First, a correction: **Norra isn't in PC League of Legends.** Patch 26.18 (Sept 10) added no new champion. It was a balance patch, and Syndra was one of the champions nerfed ([26.18 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes/)). The only new PC champion in 2026 is Locke, an AP assassin who came out in 26.13 ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/78585-locke-the-ashen-exorcist-arrives-in-league-of-legends-patch-26-13)). Norra is **Wild Rift's** first exclusive champion. She has been out since Feb 4, 2026 (WR patch 7.0b), and no PC release has been announced ([WR wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/WR:Norra), [Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/us/lol/articles/norra-first-exclusive-wild-rift-champion-released/en)). If you play Wild Rift, the matchup advice is below. If you play on PC, tell me who you're actually facing. Also note that PC Syndra was nerfed in 26.18: her W cooldown went up by 1s and her passive restores less mana ([V26.18](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.18)).

**Norra's kit and power spikes (Wild Rift):**
- **Q:** a bolt she charges for up to 2.5s. Its range grows from 800 to 1,200, a full charge does about 40% more damage, and it hops her back 300 units. She is slowed 20% while charging, or 30% if she keeps holding it. She maxes Q first (cooldown 7.5s down to 4.5s).
- **E:** slows you 20% and makes your hitbox bigger for 2s. Her Q then adds 1–2.5% of your max HP as damage. From level 2, E and Q together are her main trade.
- **W:** a 900-range banish. For 2.25s you can't attack or cast and you're slowed 80%. Cooldown is 23–20s. This is both her kill tool and her escape.
- **Passive:** she drops trinkets on the ground. Stepping on one damages you and reveals you.
- **Spikes:** level 2 (E+Q), **level 5** when she gets her ult, and her first finished item. The ult has 1,500–2,500 range and fires four volleys, with the fourth hitting for 140%, which makes her dangerous when roaming or skirmishing ([guide](https://gamingonphone.com/action/wild-rift-norra-build-guide-skill-analysis-best-runes-and-gameplay-tips/)).

**How to lane against her on Syndra:**
1. Stand outside the range she can hit with a quick cast. That forces her to charge every Q for longer where you can see it, so sidestep it. When she starts charging she is slowed, so walk up and land Q into E. She has no dash.
2. If her E hits you, back off right away. The charged Q she throws next is where her damage comes from.
3. Play around her W. Don't commit while she has it. Once she uses it you have about 20 seconds to all-in her with E stun and your ult.
4. From level 5, ping her ult and her roams, then push your wave and follow her.
5. She is immobile and fragile, so ask your jungler to gank her. If you'd rather counterpick, the usual answers are Kassadin, Ekko, Zoe and Ahri ([Wildriftcounter](https://wildriftcounter.com/champions/norra/), [1v9](https://1v9.gg/blog/best-champions-to-counter-norra)).

## Q2
**What finishing the mid quest gives you now:**
- **+8% bonus AD and +8% AP.** These are multipliers, so they get bigger with every item you buy. Patch 26.9 removed the old 4-second Empowered Recall and replaced it with this bonus at 6%. Patch 26.11 raised it to 8% ([V26.09](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.09), [V26.11](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.11)).
- **Free tier-3 boots.** Your tier-2 boots upgrade into a version only mid laners can get:
  - Sorcerer's → Spellslinger's Shoes
  - Lucidity → Crimson Lucidity
  - Berserker's → Gunmetal Greaves
  - Swiftness → Swiftmarch
  - Mercury's → Chainlaced Crushers
  - Steelcaps → Armored Advance
  - Gluttonous Greaves → Immortal Path

**How the quest fills (1,350 points)** ([Role Quests wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quests)):
- **Passive:** 1.5 points per second while you're in lane, or 0.33 per second outside it. Passive points alone finish the quest at about **15:08**.
- **In lane:** 2 per minion, 40 per turret plate, 50 per turret. Each of these is worth less outside your lane.
- **Anywhere:**
  - 25 per champion takedown.
  - 30 per epic monster (drake, Void Grubs, Herald), with no penalty for location.
  - 1.5% of the damage you deal to champions if you're ranged, or 3% if you're melee.
- **Penalties:**
  - Finishing a recall stops passive points for 12s.
  - Being out of lane cuts your gains by 75%, and that penalty shrinks as the quest progresses.
  - Every 2 minutes you spend in lane banks 1 minute of roaming with no penalty.

**Fastest way to finish it:**
1. Stay in lane and don't miss CS. 100 CS is about 200 points.
2. Push the wave and hit plates. Each plate is 40 points plus gold.
3. Trade with the enemy mid, because damage counts. Melee champions earn points twice as fast from damage.
4. Only roam when you have banked grace time, or when you're going to drake or grubs. The 30 points from an objective aren't reduced.
5. Recall rarely, and only after your wave crashes. Every recall costs about 18 passive points plus the walk back.
6. Own tier-2 boots before you finish so the upgrade happens immediately.

Using these point values, I estimate a clean lane finishes around 12–14 minutes. That is my own calculation, not an official number.

## Q3
Teleport is still worth taking in some situations, but the top quest changed the math. The top quest needs 1,200 points, which takes about 13:35 from passive points alone and less with plates and kills. What you get for finishing depends on your summoners ([Role Quests wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quests), [26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)):
- **If you didn't take TP:** you get a free Unleashed Teleport in the quest slot, with a 390s cooldown (it was 420s before 26.19). So Flash + Ignite players still get TP. They just don't have it for roughly the first 13 minutes, and its cooldown is longer.
- **If you took TP:** your Teleport already upgrades to Unleashed at 10:00 ([Teleport wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Teleport)). Finishing the quest also cuts its cooldown to 300–210s and gives you a shield worth 35% of your max HP for 10s after you land.
- **Either way:** your level cap rises to 20, and you get +600 XP, +11% XP from everything, and +80 XP per takedown.

In 26.19, Riot said the quest "has been quite successful at freeing up top laners to skip Teleport," and buffed both versions of TP. In Emerald+ on 26.19 the choice is genuinely split, and the win rates are almost the same:

| Champion | Flash + Teleport | Flash + Ignite |
|---|---|---|
| [Aatrox](https://lolalytics.com/lol/aatrox/build/) | ~163k games, 51.8% | ~122k games, 52.3% |
| [Ornn](https://lolalytics.com/lol/ornn/build/) | ~10k games, 54.1% | ~18k games, 54.5% |

So decide based on your plan for the game.

**Take TP when:**
- **You're the weak side, a scaling pick, or in a losing or ranged matchup.** TP gets you back to lane quickly, which saves CS and XP. It also protects your quest progress, because time spent recalling and out of lane earns fewer points.
- **Your value comes from fights before about 13 minutes.** Examples are drakes and grubs on tanks and engage champions like Ornn, Shen, Sion, Malphite or K'Sante, or a jungle and bot lane that play for early drakes.
- **You're a split-pusher** like Fiora, Camille, Jax or Yorick. The upgraded 300–210s TP with a shield is much better than the 390s free one.

**Take Ignite when:**
- you have real kill pressure from levels 2–6 (Darius, Renekton, Pantheon, Riven, Irelia, Sett, Olaf);
- the enemy laner or jungler heals a lot (Aatrox, Vladimir, Mundo, Warwick);
- you're playing to snowball off dives with your jungler.

**Other options:**
- **Ghost:** juggernauts that need to reach and stick to targets (Darius, Garen, Nasus, Mordekaiser, Udyr).
- **Exhaust:** scaling champions facing burst duelists like Riven, Irelia, Yone or Fiora.

## Q5
These are the biggest changes to Summoner's Rift since Worlds 2025:

1. **Role quests for every role (26.1).** Each role fills a quest by staying in lane, farming, taking plates and kills, and joining objectives. Leaving lane early slows your quest, which changed how and when people roam ([26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)). Rewards by role:
   - **Top:** level cap 20, bonus XP, and a free or upgraded Teleport.
   - **Mid:** +8% AD/AP and tier-3 boots.
   - **Bot:** extra gold, and boots move to the quest slot, which frees an item slot.
   - **Support:** cheaper control wards and a slot just for wards.
   - **Jungle:** the pet evolves sooner, plus bonus move speed in the jungle and extra gold/XP per large monster.
2. **Faster games and different objectives** ([V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01)):
   - Atakhan, Blood Roses and Feats of Strength have been removed.
   - Minions spawn at **0:30** (it was 1:05) and jungle camps at about 0:55. From 14:00 a wave comes every 25s, and from 30:00 every 20s.
   - **Baron spawns at 20:00** and gives less gold.
   - Drakes are tankier and worth 75g. More of the Void Grub and Herald rewards go to whoever kills them.
   - New **Faelights** spots boost the vision of wards placed on them.
3. **Turrets:**
   - Outer turrets have 9,000 HP but lose resistances from 11:00.
   - Plates are now permanent and also appear on inner and inhibitor turrets. Turrets give no local gold, and first turret is worth 300g.
   - **Crystalline Overgrowth** builds up on turrets and turns into a burst of true damage when a champion hits one. It works like Demolish for everyone.
4. **Items and runes:**
   - **26.1:** crit damage is back to 200%. New items include Actualizer, Fiendhunter Bolts, Bastionbreaker and Dusk and Dawn. Luden's is called Luden's Echo again.
   - **Season 2 (26.9):** Phase Rush was replaced by Stormraider's Surge, and Deathfire Touch came back. New items: Doran's Bow, Doran's Helm and Gluttonous Greaves ([Season 2 summary](https://www.aussyelo.com/blog/lol-season-2-pandemonium-2026)).
   - **August:** Sundered Sky was nerfed twice.
5. **Champions:** two new ones, Zaahen (late 2025) and Locke (mid lane assassin, 26.13). Bel'Veth was reworked in 26.15.
6. **Ranked:**
   - There are now **three ranked seasons a year**. Season 3 started July 29 with no rank reset ([Hexgate](https://hexgate.app/blog/lol-season-3-2026-guide/)).
   - Autofilled players are matched against each other where possible, and **Aegis of Valor** gives double LP for a win while autofilled.
   - Dodging no longer gets you out of autofill.
   - WASD movement controls are allowed in ranked.
   - Grandmaster and Challenger players can't queue as a duo ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).

The Worlds patch, 26.20, goes live on Oct 7.

## Q6
**What it is:** League Classic is a free mode inside your normal client. It launched on July 29, 2026 in patch 26.15. It recreates League from around Season 3, mixing in favorites from other early seasons rather than copying one exact patch ([Riot dev blog](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-league-of-legends-classic/), [26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)). That means:
- the old Summoner's Rift look and interface;
- champions' original kits from before their reworks;
- old runes and masteries, and old items such as Heart of Gold;
- slower projectiles and more point-and-click stuns.

The queues are one PvP draft queue, Co-op vs AI, and custom games.

**What carries over to your normal account:**
- **Champions you own** in normal League can be played in Classic right away. Anything else you unlock through Classic Levels, the Classic shop, or the free rotation.
- **Skins:** many of the skins you already own work in Classic, and Riot is adding more over time.
- **Rewards:** Classic Levels and the seasonal Classic Pass give Blue Essence. They also give IP, a currency that only exists in Classic, plus runes, rune pages and cosmetics.
- **Rank:** nothing carries over. Classic has no ranked queue and doesn't affect your Summoner's Rift rank or MMR. It has its own ladder called Summoner's Journey (Salt → Wood → … → Legend), which starts at Classic Level 10 ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/78785-league-of-legends-classic-release-date-champions-classic-pass-and-the-council-revealed)).
- **Missions:** I couldn't confirm whether Classic games count toward missions in the normal game.

## Q7
You're not imagining it. Sundered Sky was nerfed in two patches in a row:
- **26.16 (Aug 12).** Riot called it "the meta dominant AD fighter item for the last year," saying it was healing for more than lifesteal items ([26.16 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)).
  - The heal from its empowered hit (Lightshield Strike) dropped from 100% to 90% of base AD for melee champions, and from 6% to 4% of missing health.
  - Health went from 400 to 450 to make up for part of that.
  - The recipe became Tunneler + Caulfield's + 900g. The total cost is still 3,100g.
- **26.17 (Aug 25).** Riot said the first nerf "landed a bit under-target." Health went back from 450 to 400, and AD dropped from 45 to 40 ([26.17 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/), [item history](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sundered_Sky)).

Compared with early August, that is 5 less AD, the same 400 HP, and a third less healing from missing health. You feel that most in long fights at low HP.

**26.19 then adjusted the champions:** Aatrox got a shorter W cooldown and more healing from his E. Riot said directly that this was because of the Sundered Sky nerfs. Vi was nerfed (base AD 63→61 and her passive shield 12%→10% of max HP) because she was still strong without the item ([26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)).

**What fighters build now.** 26.16 also buffed the alternatives ([V26.16](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.16)):
- Black Cleaver: 40 → 45 AD.
- Eclipse: damage 6% → 8% for melee.
- Sterak's Gage: 45% → 50% of base AD.
- Tiamat: 20 → 25 AD.

Current Emerald+ builds on 26.19:
- **Vi (jungle):** Trinity Force, then Plated Steelcaps, then Black Cleaver against tanks or Sundered Sky against squishy teams. After that, Sterak's, Death's Dance or Guardian Angel ([Lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/vi/build/), [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/champions/vi/build)).
- **Aatrox (top):** Eclipse, then Steelcaps, then Spear of Shojin. Sundered Sky is still an option as his third item. Later, Sterak's or Serylda's and Death's Dance ([Lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/aatrox/build/)).

In short, fighters now buy Sundered Sky second or third for sustain in long fights instead of rushing it first. Black Cleaver, Eclipse and Trinity Force cover the early power spike. The 26.20 preview doesn't change Sundered Sky.

## Q8
**Play these games out. Dodging is now strictly worse:**
- **Dodging doesn't get you out of autofill.** Since 26.1, your autofill status carries over to the next lobby ([ranked changes](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/league-of-legends-ranked-2026-changes-explained-autofill-updates-aegis-of-valor-dodging-rules-and-faster-queue-times-asZ9b4U04yVM)).
  - So you pay the dodge penalty and then get autofilled again anyway.
  - Penalty: the first dodge in 24 hours costs 5 LP and a 6-minute lockout. The second costs 15 LP and 30 minutes. After that, 15 LP and 12 hours ([wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Queue_dodging)).
  - In Master and above, dodging an autofilled lobby counts as a full loss.
- **Aegis of Valor (current rules since 26.15).** When the lobby tells you Aegis is active, winning while autofilled gives **double LP**. Losing costs your normal LP ([26.15 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)).
  - Patch 26.15 removed both the old loss protection and the mastery-grade requirement.
  - Aegis only shows up sometimes. It isn't guaranteed every time you're autofilled.
  - You lose it if you swap roles in champion select or get penalized for something like going AFK ([Riot support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/matchmaking-and-autofill)).
- **Matching:** the game tries to put you against an autofilled player in the same role. If it can't, it gives each team the same number of autofilled players. If that fails too, the team with more autofilled players gets slightly higher-MMR teammates.
- **Autofill protection:** after you've been autofilled, you're guaranteed your primary or secondary role "for the next handful of games." Riot doesn't publish the exact number.
- **Priority roles:** players who queue Support or Jungle get Aegis rewards at about the same rate as autofilled players, just without the notice. Choosing **Fill** means you can never be autofilled.

**Practical tips:**
- Keep 2–3 easy supports ready. Strong, low-difficulty picks in Emerald+ on 26.19 ([Lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/tierlist/?lane=support)):

  | Support | Win rate |
  |---|---|
  | Janna | 52.6% |
  | Leona | 53.9% |
  | Sona | 52.9% |
  | Nami | 52.1% |
  | Alistar | 52.6% |

- Don't trade roles in a lobby where Aegis is active.
- If support keeps coming up, try Jungle as your secondary role. You'd still get a role you picked, and Jungle is one of the priority roles that can earn Aegis bonuses.
