## What an expert at 7k+ offlane must know (M as principles)
- Lane: manage the safelane creep equilibrium, contest/deny pulls, harass the carry, secure levels; win, tie or survive by hero.
- Timings: first core item (Blink/Vanguard/Blade Mail) by ~12–15 min; play around Tormentor/Roshan and power runes.
- Aura/utility items for team (Pipe/Crimson/Vlads) vs self items; initiation vs frontline roles.
- Drafting: counterpick the enemy safelane, flex picks, last-pick offlane.
- Common high-MMR mistakes: farming too long, bad Blink initiations, no buyback awareness.
