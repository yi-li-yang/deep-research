## OpenDota reproduction
Explorer base: `https://api.opendota.com/api/explorer?sql=<URL-encoded SQL>`. "Offlane" means `lane_role = 3`. The offlaner is picked per team with `DISTINCT ON (match_id, is_radiant)`.

- **Q1, 7.41f table.** Joins `player_matches` to `matches`, with `start_time >= 1789455600` (2026-09-15 07:00 UTC), `start_time < 1791385615` (2026-10-07 15:06:55 UTC), `leagueid IN (20279, 19102, 20393)`, and `ORDER BY ... net_worth DESC NULLS LAST`. Win is `is_radiant = radiant_win`.
  - Leagues: 20279 = PGL Wallachia 2026 Season 9, 19102 = "BLAST SLAM VIII", 20393 = PARI Universe by FISSURE Closed Qualifiers.
  - Without the upper bound the query returns 200 matches, because three PARI qualifier matches were played between 15:06 and 18:02 UTC. Then Doom is 46 (50%), Centaur 37 (51), Largo 30 (60) and Necrophos 22 (55). The briefing's figures are a snapshot taken before about 15:00 UTC.
- **Q2, nine-event table.** The same query with `ORDER BY last_hits DESC`, `start_time` from 1774483200 to 1791072000, and `leagueid IN (19696, 19785, 19719, 19543, 20279, 19101, 19102, 20009, 19422)`.
  - The briefing does not name the nine events. I found them by searching subsets of per-league aggregates. Exactly one set matched all 11 figures (970 matches plus the five hero games and wins).
  - The set: DreamLeague S29, EWC 2026, TI 2026, PGL Wallachia S8, PGL Wallachia S9, BLAST SLAM VII, BLAST SLAM VIII, 1win Essence II and ESL One Birmingham 2026.
- **Q3, items and builds.** Uses `unnest(purchase_log)` with `e->>'key'`, `min((e->>'time')::int)` and `percentile_cont(0.5)`, over the same 1,940 offlaner team-games as Q2.
- **Q4, lane partners and pick order.** Counts lane-role-3 players per team, and ranks the offlaner's hero among `unnest(picks_bans)` picks by `order`.
- **Q5, medians.** The first Tier 1 tower is the minimum `building_kill` time with a `tower1` key. The first Roshan is `CHAT_MESSAGE_ROSHAN_KILL`. Game length is `duration`.
