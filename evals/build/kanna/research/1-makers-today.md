## Checked beliefs

- **CHANGED: "Production centres are Miki (largest), Yoita, Sanjō and historic Tokyo."** Miki is still slightly ahead of Yoita, then Sanjō, but every centre is small.
  - A 2019–21 field survey counted 8 kanna smiths in the Miki area (including Ono and Nishiwaki), 6 in Yoita (2 of them trainees) plus Japan's only 2 small-plane (小鉋) specialists, and 4 in Sanjō.
  - Sanjō now matters mainly for plane bodies (dai): it had 10 of the 14 dai makers surveyed and is the only source of small-plane dai.
  - [databook](https://www.forest.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/gifu_tools_databook2020.pdf) — as of March 2021.
  - Tokyo is close to extinct as a plane centre. Dealers mark almost all Tokyo makers 廃業 (closed). 金山鉄夫 Kanayama Tetsuo (Tokyo-trained, working in Toyama) closed in 2024. 神田規久夫 Kanda Kikuo (も作 Mosaku) stopped because of age.
  - [Hokuto](http://www5e.biglobe.ne.jp/~ttoishi/sub44.htm) — 2024.
  - One Tokyo forge, 八重樫打刃物製作所 Yaegashi (mei 宗秋 Muneaki), still makes plane blades. [Kurashige](https://shop.kurashige-tools.com/collections/muneaki-%E5%AE%97%E7%A7%8B) — November 2025.

- **CONFIRMED, and the chain has more specialists than the belief says: "a plane is a joint product."**
  - In Miki the route is smith → 裏押屋 (back-flattening and sharpening shop) → 台屋 (dai maker), and many smiths still ship bare blades. [Yamamoto](http://yamamotokanna.sakura.ne.jp/kaji4dai.html) — 2019.
  - Specialist chipbreaker (押金) smiths are gone. Miki has none, the Sanjō supplier closed around 2014, and smiths now make their own. Special dai for Miki planes (際 rebate, 台直し) now come from Sanjō.
  - 常三郎 Tsunesaburō uses three outside dai shops (松尾木工, オーク製作所, にむら木工 Nimura) plus an in-house fitter. [Tsunesaburō](https://www.tsune36.co.jp/company/daiuchi/) — 2026.

- **CONFIRMED: "Mei are often brand or dealer names, and famous names are reused."**
  - 王道 Ōdō passed from 山田清作 Yamada Seisaku (died 2004) to 水野清介 Mizuno Seisuke in 2004.
  - 神技 Kamiwaza is a Niigata dealer's brand, made by 中野武夫 Nakano Takeo. [dealer](https://daikuhamono.sakura.ne.jp/kanna.htm) — March 2026.
  - A Niigata wholesaler, 田中政八商店, stamped planes 千代鶴.

- **CONFIRMED: Chiyozuru Korehide 千代鶴是秀 (1874–1957)**, born 加藤廣 and trained under the Ishidō smiths. [Chiyozuru Sadahide site](https://chiyozurusadahide.jp/shokunin)
  - The Miki line he gave his name to has had three holders of the name 千代鶴貞秀 Chiyozuru Sadahide: 神吉義良 Kanki Yoshiyoshi (1949), 神吉岩雄 Kanki Iwao (1990) and 森田直樹 Morita Naoki (February 2019).
  - Iwao's 1990 succession was decided by third-party evaluation, not by inheritance.

- **PARTLY WRONG: the revered 20th-century names.**
  - **Usui Kengo 碓氷健吾** (Yoita) was born 28 September 1928 and died 26 March 2012. His mei include 健明, 寿一, 明寿, 序乃舞 and 夫婦鉋. [dealer](https://daikuhamono.sakura.ne.jp/sankoukanna.htm)
  - No source I found links him to 清忠 Kiyotada. Kurashige lists Kiyotada as the wholesaler 水平屋's mark, smith unknown. [Kurashige](https://shop.kurashige-tools.com/collections/kiyotada) — 2024. **Contested.**
  - **Tasai 田斎** is a working Sanjō forge, mostly chisels, not a historical name: 田齋明夫 Akio (born 1939) and his son 道生 Michio (born 1969). Lead time was 1–2 years as of June 2025. [Kurashige](https://shop.kurashige-tools.com/collections/tasai-%E7%94%B0%E6%96%8E)
  - **Ishidō 石堂**: the 10th-generation 輝秀 Teruhide (菊池清一) is dead; the 11th is 秀雄 Hideo; one dealer names 良孝 Yoshitaka as 12th. What is on sale is mostly old stock. Whether they are producing now is unclear.

- **The current retail names:**
  - **Tsunesaburō — CONFIRMED active.** Third-generation head 魚住徹 Uozumi Tōru (born 1959), with staff born 1984 and 1994. Products include HAP40 powder-steel 酒壺 (Miki) planes and a new Swedish-steel model 汐音 (November 2025). Its president chaired the 42nd national Kezuroukai meet in Miki in June 2026. [Tsunesaburō](https://www.tsune36.co.jp/) — 2026.
  - **Funahiro 舟弘 — CHANGED.** 船津祐司 Funatsu Yūji died 13 March 2026, aged 81 by the Japanese count; the Yoita guild lists the forge as closed. [dealer](https://daikuhamono.sakura.ne.jp/sankoukanna.htm); [risaku](https://risakuworks.official.ec/categories/6127805)
  - **Chiyozuru Sadahide — CONFIRMED.** Morita Naoki (born 1978, from Gifu, not family) has held the name since 2019.
  - **Kiyohisa 清久 — CONFIRMED, but mainly chisels.** Maker is 渡辺清栄 Watanabe Kiyoe, Sanjō, Shirogami #1; new stock May 2026. [Hokuto](http://www5e.biglobe.ne.jp/~ttoishi/sub59.html)
  - **Kunihiro — unresolved.**

- **CONFIRMED, with figures: "smiths are aging with few apprentices."**
  - The 20 kanna smiths surveyed averaged 59.6 years old.
  - Yoita's active smiths averaged 67.8, and its youngest traditional smith was in his 60s.
  - Dai makers averaged 66; 57% had no successor, and in Miki none did.
  - Miki is the healthiest: average 57, and every smith there is either established or has a successor. [databook](https://www.forest.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/gifu_tools_databook2020.pdf) — 2021.
  - Overseas demand: orders from the US, Canada and Taiwan, and more foreigners at Kezuroukai. [Yamamoto](http://yamamotokanna.sakura.ne.jp/kaji4dai.html) — 2019.

## New findings

- **A new smith.** 似鳥透 Nitori Tōru (born 1989, a former Nikon engineer) apprenticed with Mizuno from October 2020.
  - A citizens' group ran crowdfunding (¥1.116M) and the city paid a wage subsidy; the subsidy capped training at 5 years.
  - He made about 1,000 planes and went independent in October 2025 as 水鳥刃物 Mizudori Hamono, still using Mizuno's workshop.
  - Lines: 朱雀 Suzaku (wrought-iron backing), 羽根印 (mild-steel economy line), plus wholesaler mei 初鳥 and 越鳥 (for 石弘), 小鳥 and 龍飛. [Mizudori](https://mizudori-hamono.com/about/profile/) — October 2025.
- **The Yoita guild.** Of its 5 member forges, 3 are now marked closed: Funahiro, 中野鑿 (Nakano chisels) and 碓氷刃物 (high-speed-steel chisels). Still active: 水野鉋製作所 Mizuno and 小森小鉋製作所 Komori.
  - Yoita had about 300 smithies in the Taishō era and 117 guild members in 1927; it now has about 10 forges and 4 certified craftsmen. A second trainee, 島田 Shimada, started in 2017. [guild](https://yoita-uchihamono.com/) — 2026.
  - The Yoita knife-craft hall (与板刃物工芸館) closed on 28 February 2025. [Niigata Nippo](https://www.niigata-nippo.co.jp/articles/-/562988)
- **Mizuno Seisuke** (about 70 in 2020–21, a pupil of Usui) makes seven mei: 和楽 (Tōgō steel salvaged from a Yamagata saw smith), 倭心 (Swedish steel), 我逢人 (K120), 紫清 (White #1), 青壱 (Blue #1), 知足 and 百年刻. [guild](https://yoita-uchihamono.com/master/)
- **Small planes depend on one family:** 小森秀樹 Komori Hideki (born 1952) and his brother 正樹 Masaki, with no successor as of 2021. Nakano Takeo of 中野かんな製作所 (forging since 1959) is still producing in 2026.
- **Miki's certified kanna craftsmen in 2019:** Kanki Iwao, Morita Naoki and 山本芳博 Yamamoto Yoshihiro (鉄心斎芳楽). His son 山本健介 Kensuke has since been certified. [list](http://www.kougeishi.jp/list_by_kougeihin_kougeihin_id-187-mode-gyoushu_kensaku.html)
  - Miki had 55–87 kanna smithies between 1929 and 1939, and 32 dai makers in 1961. [Yamamoto](https://yamamotokanna.sakura.ne.jp/mikinokannnakajinorekisibunsyou.html)
- **内橋圭介 Uchihashi Keisuke** (mei 圭三郎 Keisaburō, Nishiwaki) trained five years with a Miki smith before going independent; he had a 2-year order backlog per Tokyo dealer 土田刃物店. [MetalOne](https://www.mtlo.co.jp/special/metalculture/2210/) — winter 2020.
- **Dai makers named on dealer listings:** 小吉屋 Koyoshiya (渡辺光雄 Watanabe Mitsuo, Sanjō), 猪本 Inomoto, 梨屋 Nashiya and 西村祐一 Nishimura Yūichi. A new name, 山本禎恒 Yamamoto Yoshitsune ("きへん"), appears in 2025 (unverified).
- **Kezuroukai 削ろう会:** led by 谷和雄 Tani Kazuo since May 2021; its secretariat moved to Hadano in April 2024. [site](https://kezuroukai.jp/)
