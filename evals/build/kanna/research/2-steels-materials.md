## Checked beliefs
- **CONFIRMED, with an update:** YSS 白紙 Shirogami and 青紙 Aogami come from the former Hitachi Metals, renamed Proterial (プロテリアル) on 4 Jan 2023 under the Bain consortium's company K.K. BCJ-52. From 1 Jul 2026 (moved from 1 Apr) that company itself trades as Proterial, Ltd. Specialty steel, which includes YSS, now sits in the 航空＆産業アロイ (Aero & Industrial Alloy) business unit. Magnets and cable became subsidiaries. The stated goal is to list the company on the stock market again. — [Dec 2025](https://www.proterial.com/e/press/2025/pdf/20251215en.pdf), [Feb 2026](https://www.proterial.com/press/2026/pdf/20260226jp.pdf), [Jul 2026](https://www.proterial.com/press/2026/pdf/2026070103jp.pdf) — 1 Jul 2026. Premium makers now also use Sandvik/ASSAB, HAP40 and old stock (see below).
- **CONFIRMED (carbon) / WRONG (Mo):** Carbon for 白紙1/2/3号 is 1.25–1.35 / 1.05–1.15 / 0.80–0.90%. 青紙1号 is Cr 0.30–0.50, W 1.50–2.00; 青紙2号 is Cr 0.20–0.50, W 1.00–1.50. The catalog gives 青紙スーパー C 1.40–1.50, Cr 0.30–0.50, W 2.00–2.50 and V 0.30–0.50, but **no Mo**. Its only Mo entry (0.30–0.50) belongs to 銀1 Gin-1. Dealer tables that list Mo for Aogami Super probably misread that table. This rests on my reading of where values sit in the PDF table, and the current Proterial catalog is behind a request form. — [Hitachi catalog](https://knifesteelnerds.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Hitachi-cutlery-steel.pdf) — Hitachi-era catalog, posted 2020.
- **UNVERIFIED (lore):** The catalog's main-use column lists all three 青紙 grades for 最高級鉋 (finest planes) and 白紙1号 for 最高級付刃物・鉋・のみ. Lab tests on knife steels, not planes, found Blue Super has coarser, uneven carbides and less toughness than 26C3. Larrin Thomas treats 26C3 as similar to White #1. — [KSN](https://knifesteelnerds.com/2020/02/17/ranking-toughness-of-forging-knife-steels/) — Feb 2020. A dealer argues Aogami's real advantage is easier, cheaper heat treatment, and that White #1 sharpens finer. That is opinion, not test data. — [Covington](https://covingtonandsons.com/2021/02/27/the-story-of-a-few-steels/) — updated Aug 2025.
- **UNVERIFIED:** blades at HRC 62–65. The catalog only guarantees ≥60 HRC after a 180–220 °C temper.
- **CHANGED (dealer claim):** 極軟鉄 gokunantetsu (0.04–0.07% C) is "not produced anymore", and smiths work from stockpiles. Niigata plane smiths use wrought iron bought in one lot from a demolished Yokohama bridge. Hyogo jigane comes from old anchor chains. — [Covington](https://covingtonandsons.com/2019/09/14/sharpening-part-8-soft-steel/) — 2019, updated Aug 2023. Planes on sale now still advertise 19th-century bridge iron (Nakano Takeo, [Fine Tools](https://www.fine-tools.com/Smoothing-Plane-TORYO-HOMARE-with-White-Oak-Body/310823-main)) and old temple nails, 和釘 wakugi (Funahiro, [Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2799)) — undated listings, seen Oct 2026.
- **CONFIRMED + updated:** 日刀保たたら Nittōho tatara (Okuizumo, Shimane) runs 3 times each winter. Each run uses about 10 t 砂鉄 (iron sand) and 12 t 木炭 (charcoal), gives about 3 t 鉧 (bloom) and about 1 t 玉鋼. — [Japan Heritage](https://japan-heritage.bunka.go.jp/ja/special/135/) — Jun 2024; [ANA](https://tsubasa.ana.co.jp/travel/dom/okuizumo202404/okuizumo07/) — Apr 2024. As a rule only licensed swordsmiths can get it, once a year. — [NBTHK](https://www.touken.or.jp/employment/tamahagane.html). The murage (tatara master) 木原明 Kihara Akira retired at the end of April 2024 ([Chugoku](https://www.chugoku-np.co.jp/articles/-/468634)) and died 22 Jun 2024 ([Proterial](https://www.proterial.com/press/2024/n0628.html)). 堀尾薫 Horio Kaoru, a Proterial Yasugi employee, was appointed 18 Jul 2024 — [Proterial](https://www.proterial.com/e/press/2024/n0724.html). Proterial still supports the tatara, as it has since 1977 — [Sep 2026](https://www.proterial.com/press/2026/n0924.html).
- **PARTLY CONFIRMED:** "Swedish" steel is often old stock. Funahiro's "Genmyo" plane uses "old Sandvik steel inherited from his late master Usui" ([Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2759)). Other planes use Sandvik (Tsunesaburo "Shizuku", [Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2801)) or ASSAB (Funahiro "Josui", $1,408, [Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2873)) — undated listings.
- **PARTLY CONFIRMED:** A mainstream Miki maker, 常三郎 Tsunesaburo, sells the "Miki" plane with a HAP40 powder-HSS blade. It is tempered around 550 °C and has a flat back with no 裏すき (hollow). Dealers claim it lasts longer than 青紙1号 and is "twice the toughness" of blue steel. Neither claim comes from a controlled test. Hida lists it out of stock. — [Fine Tools](https://www.fine-tools.com/MIKI-Smoothing-Plane/323937-main), [Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2807). For replaceable blades, Kakuri 角利 lists a 替刃式ホビー鉋 (kaeba-shiki hobby plane). I did not check carbide.
- **CONFIRMED:** The 台 (dai, plane body) is mainly シラカシ shirakashi or アカガシ akagashi ([ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%89%8B)). Hida sells blanks of "Japanese white oak dried for more than 10 years" ([Hida](https://hidatool.com/item/2313)), and Kakuri uses air-dried (自然乾燥) Japanese oak. I did not verify isu.

## New findings
- **New YSS grade, 藍紙® Aigami (9 Dec 2025):** Proterial calls it "neither carbon nor stainless". 藍紙2号 is in mass production at Yasugi, with hardness at or above 青紙2号 and corrosion resistance close to GIN3. 藍紙1号 is still in trial. The composition is not disclosed. Targets are kitchen knives, scalpels, razor blades and outdoor knives; planes are not mentioned. It is probably the YBS1/YBS2 steel announced 15 Apr 2024, since the benchmarks match (my inference). — [Proterial](https://www.proterial.com/press/2025/n1209.html), [2024](https://www.proterial.com/press/2024/n0415.html)
- **Yasugi reorganized internally (1 Apr 2025):** Proterial absorbed プロテリアル安来製作所, which did cold processing, inspection and raw-material management at Yasugi. — [JMD](https://www.japanmetaldaily.com/articles/-/240324)
- **Sales of other units:** Waupaca (Dec 2023), Ward (Feb 2024), the piping business (Mar 2024) and automotive castings (slated for Jan 2026) were sold. None of these touch YSS. — [Proterial press list](https://www.proterial.com/press/2024/)
- **Rumors about YSS supply (contested):** Proterial issued no discontinuation or price notice in 2023–2026, and its cutlery page still lists 青紙, 黄紙 and 白紙 ([page](https://www.proterial.com/products/tool_steel/highclass_cutlery_steels.html), seen Oct 2026).
  - A kitchen-knife forum relayed a blacksmith's account of a ~250% price rise from a wholesaler and a phase-out of 白紙3号 and 黄紙, around 2023. The forum blocked me, so I saw only search snippets. — [KKF](https://www.kitchenknifeforums.com/threads/yasugi-steel-price-increase-250.69416/)
  - Covington, a dealer, says Hitachi "is no longer producing" White #1/#2 and Blue #1 (article updated Aug 2025). Proterial's Dec 2025 release still uses 青紙2号 and 白紙1号 as current comparisons, which contradicts this. A scarcity claim also helps a dealer sell.
- **Tamahagane tested (10 Sep 2025):** Knife Steel Nerds measured a swordsmith-forged tamahagane sample: 1.06% C, about 0.1% inclusions, 64.6 HRC. Its toughness and edge retention matched ordinary low-alloy steels like 26C3, with no advantage. — [KSN](https://knifesteelnerds.com/2025/09/10/testing-tamahagane-traditional-japanese-steel/)
- **Ancient-style tatara in Sanjo (3 Oct 2026):** Proterial supplied operators and materials for an NBTHK-authorized reconstruction run at 相場商事, a Sanjo hardware wholesaler. What happens to the steel is not stated. — [Proterial](https://www.proterial.com/press/2026/n0916.html)
- **Marketing to discount:**
  - Covington warns that knives stamped 玉鋼 are often SK or stainless.
  - Hida says HAP40 is "made from sand steel from Shimane", and calls a Funahiro steel "very similar to Tamahagane". Both read as marketing; I found no support for either.
  - At Hida, Japanese smoothing planes are listed at about $210–$1,870.
