- **CHANGED: the 15% US tariff on Japanese goods no longer applies.**
  - The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on Feb 20 2026. A 10% Section 122 surcharge ran from Feb 24 to Jul 24 2026 ([GTA](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/from-ieepa-to-section-122)).
  - Since Jul 24 2026, Section 301 "forced-labor" tariffs apply, with Japan capped at 12.5% including normal duty ([EY](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1607-ustr-finalizes-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies-additional-tariffs-of-10-percent-or-125-percent-take-effect-24-july-2026)).
  - Planes are tariff code 8205.30 (normal duty 5% or 5.7%, per [USITC](https://hts.usitc.gov/)), so a kanna pays **12.5% in total**.
  - 8205.30 was not in the Aug 2025 steel-derivative (Section 232) additions ([FR](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-08-19/html/2025-15819.htm)). I did not check later rounds.
- **CONFIRMED and now permanent: de minimis is gone.** Customs (CBP) rules of Jun 24 2026 suspend it indefinitely under a separate legal basis. The mail portion took effect Jul 24 2026. Bona fide gifts of $100 or less stay exempt, and the statutory repeal is set for Jul 1 2027. — [FR 2026-12669](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry)
- **CHANGED: the Japan Post suspension was not brief.** It ran from Aug 27 2025 to Apr 14 2026 ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/japan-post-is-now-accepting-us-bound-packages-over-100usd-again-041526)). Current rules ([Japan Post](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/2026/0714_01_en.html)):
  - Parcels worth $100–$2,500 go only from designated post offices. The sender must prepay duty through the Zonos app and use a duty-paid label.
  - The upper limit rose from $800 to $2,500 on Jul 24 2026.
  - Documents and gifts of $100 or less can be sent from any post office.

**US mail after Oct 22 2026 (my inference, flagged).** The same [CBP rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry) bars mail shipments carrying chapter 98/99 duties, which include Section 301, from its new simplified postal entry. Formal entry is then required. A posted kanna could get slower and costlier. How Japan Post and Zonos will handle this is unknown.

**EU:**
- From Jul 1 2026 to Jul 1 2028, parcels worth €150 or less pay a flat €3 per item instead of the old duty relief ([EUR-Lex 2026/382](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0382); [tariff](https://www.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk/xi/commodities/8205300000)).
- Above €150, planes pay 3.7%, or 0% with EU–Japan trade-deal origin proof. VAT always applies.
- Japan Post asks senders not to post business-to-consumer items under €150 to AT, DK, DE, FI, FR, BE, PT or LU, because they may be returned. It suggests its courier service instead ([notice](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/2026/0701_01_en.html)).
- Buying from Dictum or Fine Tools avoids import formalities.
- Japan Post halted mail to Europe and Africa in March 2026 and resumed Mar 18 (per Buyee).

**UK:**
- Parcels worth £135 or less pay no duty; VAT is charged at checkout.
- Above £135, the UK–Japan trade deal gives 0% duty (2% otherwise), plus 20% VAT and a carrier fee — [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/goods-sent-from-abroad/tax-and-duty) — Oct 2026
