Confidence: H high, M medium, L low.

## The dialect
- Sichuanese is a Southwestern Mandarin variety (H). Main varieties: Chengdu (成都话) and Chongqing (重庆话), mutually intelligible with differences in tone contour and vocabulary (M).
- Four tones; the old entering tone merged into the second tone (阳平) in Chengdu-Chongqing speech (M). n/l merger, no retroflex initials (zh→z) for many speakers, -ing→-in, "hu-" for "f-" in some words (M).
- Vocabulary a listener meets constantly: 巴适, 安逸, 晓得, 啥子, 要得, 莫得, 摆龙门阵 (chat), 耍 (play/hang out), 雄起, 瓜娃子, 锤子, 撇脱, 打望 (H for the words, M for exact nuances).
- Romanization: Wiktionary uses a "Sichuanese Pinyin" scheme (M). Dictionaries exist (《四川方言词典》 etc.) (L on titles and editions).
