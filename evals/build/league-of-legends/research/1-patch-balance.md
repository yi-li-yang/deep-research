## New findings
- **26.19 (live Sep 23)** was described as the "first round of pro-focused" changes — [notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)
  - Buffs: Aatrox, Aphelios, Aurora, Draven, Elise, Fiora, Kha'Zix, Lillia (R sleep 2→2.5s), Volibear.
  - Lucian reshaped to depend less on enchanter supports: passive 15+20% AD → 5+15% AD, Q 80–220 → 90–250.
  - Nerfs: Nasus (Q bonus damage −10), Nocturne (R cooldown 140/115/90 → 160/130/100), Poppy (jungle clear), Rumble, Ryze, Vi (passive shield 12→10% max HP). Master Yi's Q refund is now a flat 1s.
  - Melee support items: World Atlas and Runic Compass trade health for regen (health 30/100/200 → 0/60/200; regen 25/50/75% → 50/75/75%).
  - Top role quest Teleport cooldowns: free Teleport 420→390s; upgraded Unleashed Teleport 330–240 → 300–210s.
  - Team Voice, announced for NA/OCE in 26.20, is postponed with no target patch.
- **26.18 (Sep 10)** — [notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes/)
  - Nerfs to pro supports Nautilus and Bard, plus Seraphine, Syndra and Zeri.
  - Cassiopeia reshaped to reduce her "pro skew".
  - Buffs: Ekko, Kassadin, Master Yi, Viego, Zaahen. Guinsoo's stack duration 3→4s.
  - Caps inducted into the Hall of Legends.
- **26.17 (Aug 26)** — [notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/)
  - Nerfs: Nasus and Vayne (aimed at top lane), Graves, Nocturne, Thresh, Xerath.
  - Buffs: melee carries Irelia, Qiyana, Yasuo and Yone (crit penalty −10% → −5%), plus Aurelion Sol, Cho'Gath, LeBlanc, Trundle.
  - Sundered Sky nerfed again (health 450→400, AD 45→40); Stormrazor attack speed 20→25%.
  - Arena now changes only on odd-numbered patches.
- **Context, 26.16 (Aug 12)** — [notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)
  - Early support roaming punished through the support role quest (−33% XP and gold outside bot lane until level 5).
  - Other systems: Hail of Blades nerf, ADC base MR raised to 33 + 1.1/level, jungle pet scaling shifted to help AP and tank junglers.
  - Items: Sundered Sky nerf, Runaan's buff.
- **26.20 preview (Phroxzon, Sep 29–30)** — [preview](https://x.com/RiotPhroxzon/status/2104800631001415990), [full preview](https://x.com/RiotPhroxzon/status/2105190591034462617); numbers from [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-20-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/) and corroborated by [Fanstanza](https://fanstanza.gg/lol-patch-26-20-notes-champion-buffs-nerfs/). These are preview values; the final notes may differ.
  - Phroxzon calls it the "final World's patch": the playoff meta was fine and they want to protect SoloQ.
  - Items:
    - Hextech Rocketbelt: ability haste 20→10, active damage 100+10% AP → 135+15% AP. The aim is to make it a "go-in button" and cut the durability it gives mages.
    - Experimental Hexplate: nerfed for ranged users, to allow a Vayne buff for bot lane.
    - Runaan's bolt: 65→60% AD, partly undoing the 26.16 buff.
  - Buffs:
    - Diana: W ratio 11→14% bonus HP.
    - Lillia: R sleep 2.5s → 2.5/2.75/3s.
    - Lucian: AD growth 2.5→2.9.
    - Vayne: Q mana now a flat 30, more regen.
    - Also buffed: Kennen, Mordekaiser, Neeko, Smolder (crit builds), Swain, Tahm Kench.
    - Kindred: a jungle adjustment; outlets disagree on whether it is a buff or a nerf.
  - Nerfs: Ambessa, Cassiopeia and K'Sante (called "stale" in pro play), Ashe (AD growth 3.5→3), Yunara (level 1 laning).
  - **Varus and Sylas were pulled** from the patch, although some outlets still list them.
- **2026 systems a stale prior would miss** — [26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [26.13](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-13-notes/), [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)
  - 26.1 (Jan 8) added role quests for every role, Faelights (a new vision mechanic), Crystalline Overgrowth, 200% base crit damage and new items for each class.
  - The 2026 "seasons" so far: Season 2 began in 26.9 and Season 3 in 26.15 (Jul 29).
  - The newest champion is Locke (26.13, Jun 24); none has been released since.
  - New modes: League Classic, which launched in 26.15 and changes through community "Council" votes, and ARAM: Mayhem.
- **How Riot communicates now** — [dev hub](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/):
  - Phroxzon's previews on X.
  - Patch notes, with the occasional Mid-Patch Update appended.
  - Periodic "Dev Update" videos with written TL;DW recaps, presented by Pabro, Meddler, FeralPony and Phreak (latest 2026-09-08).
  - /dev blogs.
- **After Worlds:** Phroxzon (Sep 15) said systems changes to make ranged top laners easier to play against are in progress, but they won't come before Worlds. Shaky: no target patch was given.
