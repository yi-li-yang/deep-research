## Checked beliefs
- **CHANGED (partly): three themed seasons a year, each with its own ranked split.** There are still three themed seasons. In 2026 they were S1 "For Demacia" (Jan 8, patch 26.1), S2 "Pandemonium" (Apr 29, 26.9) and S3 "Classic" (Jul 29, 26.15, running to Jan 5, 2027). S2 was deliberately short, at 6 patches instead of the usual 8. Each season is a "Ranked Season" with its own rewards and a Victorious mission (win 15 ranked games), but ranks reset only once a year, in January (5 provisional games). Riot's patch 26.14 notes said there would be "no LP or progression disruptions" between S2 and S3. The one exception was a hard reset of Master and above to Master 0 LP in NA, EUW, EUNE, BR, LAN and TR at patch 26.9. — [Annual cycle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Patch/2026_Annual_Cycle), [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [26.14](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-14-notes/), [/dev apex reset](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-apex-tier-ranked-reset/) — Jan 8 / Apr 21 / Jul 14, 2026
- **CONFIRMED: 2026 S1 was Demacia-themed.** It was "For Demacia", with a reskinned map (petricite towers, gold winged accents) and a PvE metagame called Demacia Rising. Act II started Mar 4. — [TL;DW For Demacia](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/tldw-for-demacia-dev-update/) — Jan 8, 2026
- **CONFIRMED with changes: the support item quest.** The path World Atlas → Runic Compass → Bounty of Worlds → one of five upgrades (Bloodsong, Celestial Opposition, Dream Maker, Solstice Sleigh, Zaz'Zak's Realmspike) is unchanged. It is now the Support Role Quest (800 points). Changes:
  - Passive gold went from 5 to 9g per 10s.
  - Control wards sit in the role quest slot (up to 2) and cost 40g after the quest is done.
  - Vigilant Wardstone was removed.
  - The penalty for supports farming minions was relaxed in 26.6 and removed in 26.7.
  - 26.16 added an anti-roam penalty: minion points away from bot lane are cut 33% until level 5.
  - 26.19 shifted World Atlas and Runic Compass stats from health to health regen.
  — [Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest), [26.7](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-7-notes/), [26.16](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/), [26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/) — Jan–Sep 2026
- **WRONG now: Feats of Strength.** They were removed in 26.1. First Blood now gives +100g, and the first turret gives a 300g bonus shared among nearby players. — [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) — Jan 8, 2026
- **WRONG now: Atakhan at 20:00 and Baron at 25:00.** Atakhan and Blood Roses were removed in 26.1, and Baron spawns at 20:00 again (scales with level; 100 kill + 150 global gold). Voidgrubs and Herald are still the early top-side objectives:
  - Voidgrubs spawn once at 8:00 (since 25.09) and despawn at 14:45.
  - Herald spawns at 15:00 and despawns at 19:45.
  — [Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Baron_Nashor), [Voidgrub](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Voidgrub), [Herald](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rift_Herald) — Jan 8, 2026
- **CHANGED: drakes, plating and turrets.** Drakes, Soul at 4 and Elder after Soul are all still true, with the first drake at 5:00. Changes since 26.1:
  - Drake kill gold went from 25 to 75.
  - Dragon Vengeance is now 15/30/45%.
  - Epic monsters scale with champion level instead of game time.
  - Plates no longer fall at 14:00. They are permanent and now sit on outer, inner and inhibitor turrets, and each plate's value drops 10g per minute from 11:00 (capped at −40g).
  - Turrets take 20% more damage from melee champions.
  - A new turret mechanic, Crystalline Overgrowth, gives bonus damage to whoever next hits a turret after crystals grow on it.
  - Nexus turrets respawn at 40% health.
  — [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [Dragon Soul](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Dragon_Soul) — Jan 8, 2026
- **CONFIRMED, since adjusted: role quests for every role, and Faelights.** Current role quest rewards:
  - **Top:** 1200 points. Unleashed Teleport, level cap 20, +600 XP, +11% XP from non-champion sources and +80 XP per takedown.
  - **Mid:** 1350 points. Free tier-3 boots. The original 4-second empowered recall was replaced in 26.9 by +6% bonus AD/AP, raised to 8% in 26.11.
  - **Bot:** 1350 points. +300g, +2g per minion, +40g per takedown (down from 50), and boots move into the quest slot, which works as a 7th item slot.
  - **Jungle:** 35 pet treats, then +10g/+10 XP per large monster and bonus movement speed in jungle and river.
  - 26.9 also made roaming less punishing (a "roaming bank" of allowed time away from lane) but disables passive progress for 12s after recalling.

  Faelights: a ward placed on a Faelight spot gets +25% vision radius and reveals a bonus area for 45s. Patch 26.3 nerfed them: the river reveal now stops short of the Scuttle Crab pad and epic monster pit entrances. 26.3 also rolled back the early yellow-trinket cooldown buff (level 1 is back to 210s) and removed some Scryer's Blooms. — [Role Quest wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Role_Quest), [26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/), [26.3](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-3-notes/) — Jan–Sep 2026
- **CHANGED: the eight core items.** All are still in the game, but several were changed:
  - Unending Despair is armor-only (50 armor, no MR, 15 ability haste) since 26.1.
  - Sundered Sky got a new recipe and lower healing (26.16–26.17).
  - Yun Tal Wildarrows' cost and stats were reshuffled (26.1, 26.15).
  - Experimental Hexplate was nerfed for ranged champions (26.11, 26.20).
  — [Sundered Sky](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Sundered_Sky), [Yun Tal](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Yun_Tal_Wildarrows), [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) — Jan–Oct 2026
- **CONFIRMED: ranked tiers Iron to Challenger, with Emerald.** No tier changes found. — [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) — 2026

## New findings
- **Current state.** Patch 26.19 has been live since Sep 22–23. Patch 26.20, which Riot says is the Worlds patch, releases Oct 7, 2026. S3 Act II, "Riot Records" (HEARTSTEEL skins), starts the same day; Act I, "Forgotten Lands", ends Oct 6. — [26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/), [V26.20 wiki (pre-release)](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.20), [Classic wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Classic_(Season_2026))
- **S3 "Classic" theme.** It celebrates League's "Golden Age", nostalgia and music. Its headline mode, League Classic (26.15), is unranked and gives 60 champions their classic kits plus old items such as Heart of Gold. It keeps growing: champion swapping and a player "Council" vote in 26.17, new champions added in 26.18 and 26.19. Whether it is permanent is unclear; sources disagree. — [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/) — Jul 29, 2026
- **S2 "Pandemonium" theme.** Demons on the outskirts of Demacia, centred on Vayne. New champion Locke arrived in 26.13 (Jun 24). — [Pandemonium wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Pandemonium), [Annual cycle](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Patch/2026_Annual_Cycle)
- **Patch 26.1 pace and combat.** Minions now spawn at 0:30 (was 1:05), camps at 0:55 and Scuttle at 2:55. Base crit damage went from 175% to 200% globally; the December preview had pitched it as a bot-lane quest reward instead. Lane-swap detection was deleted. — [Crit wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Critical_strike), [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)
- **Patch 26.1 items.**
  - Nine new items: Dusk and Dawn, Fiendhunter Bolts, Endless Hunger, Bastionbreaker, Actualizer, Hexoptics C44, Bandlepipes, Protoplasm Harness, and Whispering Circlet (which upgrades to Diadem of Songs). The December dev preview used placeholder names such as "Scepter of Bonking" and "Snowbow", so a source written from the preview will have the wrong names.
  - Hextech Gunblade and Stormrazor returned.
  - Aegis of the Legion and Symbiotic Soles were removed (Symbiotic Soles' role went into the mid quest).
  - Tear items became semi-unique.
  — [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [S1 preview](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-2026-season-one-gameplay-preview/)
- **Patch 26.9 items and runes.**
  - New: Doran's Bow, Doran's Helm, and Gluttonous Greaves (omnivamp boots).
  - Statikk Shiv and Voltaic Cyclosword were reworked.
  - Opportunity and Trailblazer were removed.
  - Runes: Phase Rush was removed, Deathfire Touch and Stormraider's Surge returned, and Arcane Comet was reworked.
  - 26.14 later raised Blue buff's ability haste to 10/15/20 by level.
  — [26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/), [26.14](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-14-notes/)
- **Ranked changes and walk-backs in 2026.**
  - **Aegis of Valor** (Riot's protection for autofilled players): at launch, a mastery grade of C or better meant no LP loss on a defeat or double LP on a win. 26.3 raised the grade to B. 26.15 refocused it: no loss protection any more, and double LP on wins with no grade requirement.
  - **Autofill matching:** autofilled players are matched against each other by position (EUW in 26.5, all regions in 26.7).
  - **Other additions:** a climb indicator showing when someone's MMR is above their rank (26.3), full LP refunds for victims of AFK or trolling players (26.7), and higher LP costs for Master+ dodges.
  - **High-rank duo:** 26.1 allowed duo all the way to Challenger in most regions. Riot then admitted LP "mistakes": apex players swung between +10/−30 and ±20 LP depending on the patch. It hard-reset the six regions at 26.9, with a ±30 LP baseline and cutoffs of 400 LP for Grandmaster and 800 LP for Challenger. In 26.15, Master players can duo only with others close in rank, and Grandmaster/Challenger players (or anyone with that MMR) cannot duo.
  — [/dev apex reset](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-apex-tier-ranked-reset/), [26.5](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-5-notes/), [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/)
- **WASD movement.** Added in 25.24 (Dec 3, 2025) but initially not usable in normal or ranked queues. It was enabled for Ranked, including Clash, shortly after 26.9. — [esports-news](https://esports-news.co.uk/2025/12/01/lol-2026-update-ranked-wasd-atakhan-removed-role-quests/), [26.9](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/)
- **Other modes.**
  - Swiftplay overhaul (26.1): start at level 3 with 1400g, no Grubs or Herald, Baron at 12:00, and a new "Minion Frenzy" mechanic.
  - Arena: rotating events and augment levels (26.9), with balance updates on alternating patches from 26.17.
  - ARAM: Mayhem continues.
  — [26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), [26.17](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-17-notes/)
- **"League Next" (Riot's 2027 overhaul).** Planned: an integrated client, a full visual overhaul of Summoner's Rift with gameplay changes, rune and pre-game changes, and a new player experience. It was revealed in December 2025 after a Bloomberg report. Riot says its Worlds 2026 update will not be a full reveal. — [League Next wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/League_Next)
- **Shaky source, flagged.** An esports-news.co.uk piece (Apr 22, 2026) says S2 raised First Blood and first-tower gold, cut plating income and "returned crit to 200%". None of this is in the official 26.9 notes; it looks like recycled S1 changes. — [link](https://esports-news.co.uk/2026/04/22/lol-dev-update-season-changes/)
