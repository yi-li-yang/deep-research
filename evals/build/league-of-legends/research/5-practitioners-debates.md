## New findings

- **Calendar.** 2026 has three seasons: S1 on 26.1, S2 "Pandemonium" on 26.9 (only 6 patches), S3 on 26.15 (Jul 29). Live is 26.19 (Sept 23), and Worlds will be played on 26.20 (Oct 7).
  - 26.19 shortened Teleport cooldowns (quest TP 420→390s), buffed melee-support regen, and nerfed Vi, Nocturne, Rumble and Ryze. — [26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)
  - 26.20 previews Lucian and Vayne buffs; K'Sante, Ambessa, Cassiopeia and Ashe nerfs; and ranged Hexplate/Rocketbelt nerfs. Riot is "very careful about increasing ranged top champions." — [etoday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2631349)
- **The 2026 map rewards laning, split-pushing and sieging.** — [wiki V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01)
  - First minion wave at 0:30.
  - Faelights: ward spots with bonus vision.
  - Crystalline Overgrowth: turrets left standing more than 90s take % true damage.
  - Plates on inner turrets; melee deals +20% to turrets; Nexus towers respawn at 40%.
  - LCK pros said Atakhan's removal cut forced teamfights and fewer lane swaps make laning decide more. Top laners still took TP in January. By September, Riot says the quest TP has "freed" top from a mandatory TP.
- **Emergent strategies Riot shut down:**
  - Supports roaming as second junglers (e.g. Camille). The off-lane minion penalty rose to −33% until level 5 in 26.16 (Aug 12). — [26.16](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)
  - Seraphine bot, "a source of frustration," and Bard, a "meta staple," were nerfed in 26.18.
- **Ranked, and the pitfalls the systems now police:**
  - Aegis of Valor gives autofilled players LP protection or double LP for a C grade. It became wins-only in 26.15 because players played for the grade instead of the win.
  - Duo returned to Master; GM and Challenger stay solo. This conflicts with January's "all ranks" plan.
  - Dodging in Master+ counts as a loss. — [26.15](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-15-notes/), [gameriv](https://gameriv.com/league-of-legends-2026-all-upcoming-ranked-changes/)
  - Since S2, games can end early when griefing is detected, with LP that favours the non-offending side. WASD controls are allowed in Ranked.
  - Riot's own numbers (self-reported; flag): autofill mirror-matching rose from 35% to 91%, queues are about 40% faster, and Master+ dodges fell from 18% to 3%. — [playforum](https://www.playforum.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=634879)
  - A ban now applies to all of a person's accounts, which "nearly doubl[ed] enforcement numbers in Korea." — [gameple](https://www.gameple.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=216038) — 2026-07-15
- **Vanguard:**
  - Optional On-Demand mode needs Windows 11 25H2 with Secure Boot, TPM, VBS and IOMMU; about 35% of players are eligible. — [Riot](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/vanguard-on-demand) — 2026-06-24
  - Anti-boost: 296,416 accounts actioned for rank manipulation across VALORANT and LoL. — [Riot](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/vanguard-anti-boost) — 2026-09-17
  - The often-quoted "under 1% of ranked games have a scripter" figure dates from [Aug 2024](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-vanguard-x-lol-retrospective/).
- **Client and the future:**
  - **League Next** (announced Dec 18, 2025) is planned for 2027. It brings a new integrated client, a visual overhaul of Summoner's Rift, rune and pre-game changes, and a new player experience. It is not a "LoL 2." — [hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/league-next-league-of-legends-update)
  - **League Classic** is an unranked throwback to the 2013-era Season 3 game: 60 champions, runes and masteries, and IP. It shipped with 26.15. — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/23755/lol-classic-revealed-60-champions-runes-and-masteries-return)
  - **Team Voice** (Honor 3+ needed to speak) was set for NA/OCE in 26.20 but has been postponed. — [26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)
