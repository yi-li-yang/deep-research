## New findings
**Worlds 2026** — [lolesports primer](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/worlds-2026-primer) (Oct 5 2026), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_League_of_Legends_World_Championship). Dates below are US local; KST is a day later.
- **Venues and dates:**
  - Play-In: Oct 15–18, Riot Games Arena, Los Angeles
  - Swiss: Oct 23–31, Credit Union of Texas Event Center, Allen TX
  - Quarterfinals and semifinals: Nov 3–8, Allen
  - Final: **Nov 14, Barclays Center, Brooklyn**
- **Slots:** 19 teams. Each league gets 3, except CBLOL with 2. The MSI winner's and runner-up's regions get one extra slot each, which is why the LCK and LPL have 4.
- **Seeds:**
  - LCK: Gen.G, HLE, T1, DK
  - LPL: AL, BLG, TES, IG
  - LEC: G2, MKOI, KC
  - LCS: TL, LYON, C9
  - LCP: TSW, CFO, MVK
  - CBLOL: LOS or FURIA as 1st seed, decided by their final on **Oct 10**
- **KT, the 2025 finalist, did not qualify.**
- **Play-In:** KC, C9, MVK and the CBLOL 2nd seed play a Bo5 double-elimination bracket. Only one team advances.
- **Swiss Round 1 tiers:**
  - Tier 1: GEN / AL / TL / G2
  - Tier 2: CBLOL1 / TSW / HLE / BLG
  - Tier 3: LYON / MKOI / T1 / TES
  - Tier 4: CFO / IG / DK / Play-In winner
- **Match formats:** Swiss games are Bo1, with advancement and elimination matches played Bo3. Knockouts are Bo5.
- **Status today (Oct 6):** no games played yet. The draw is on Oct 10.
- **Patch 26.20** will be the Worlds patch. Riot's 26.19 notes say: "the Worlds patch (which is the patch *after* this one)". — [Riot 26.19](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes) — Sep 22 2026
- **Patch numbering:** Riot names patches 26.x, but the client and gol.gg show 16.x (16.17 = 26.17).

**Pro meta.** I computed this from [gol.gg](https://gol.gg/champion/list/season-S16/split-ALL/tournament-LCK%202026%20Season%20Playoffs/) data, filtered by role. It covers the LCK, LPL, LEC and LCS playoffs plus the LPL regional finals: Aug 28–Oct 4, 178 games, patches 26.16–26.17. "Presence" means the champion was picked or banned in that share of games.
- **Top:** Rumble, K'Sante, **Olaf** (70 bans), Gnar, Jayce, Ambessa, Camille, Renekton. Jax is down to 12% presence.
- **Jungle:** **Nocturne** (82% presence, mostly bans), **Vi** (74%), Jarvan IV, Lee Sin, **Naafiri and Qiyana played in the jungle**, Skarner, Pantheon, Cho'Gath. Xin Zhao and Wukong are around 20%, Sejuani 6%.
- **Mid:** **Cassiopeia** at 93% presence (160 bans in 178 games), Orianna, Akali, Syndra, Ryze (the most-picked mid), Viktor, and **Locke**, a new 2026 champion. Azir (6%), Taliyah (10%) and Aurora (7%) have faded.
- **Bot:** **Yunara**, Ezreal, Ashe, Kalista, Lucian, Caitlyn, Jhin. Varus now splits between top and bot; Corki is at 9%.
- **Support:** an enchanter-heavy meta. Seraphine, Lulu, Karma, Renata and Milio lead, then Bard, Alistar, Rakan and Nautilus. Rell (8%) and Neeko (11%) have dropped.
- **At MSI and EWC (patch 26.13):** Vi, Jayce, Orianna, Poppy and Nocturne were the top priorities.
- **Nerfs since the playoffs:**
  - 26.18: Cassiopeia, Syndra, Seraphine, Nautilus, Bard. Riot wrote that Cassiopeia reached "pro play dominance despite being weaker for average players." — [Riot 26.18](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-18-notes)
  - 26.19: Nocturne, Vi, Ryze, Rumble and Poppy, explicitly aimed at pro play. It also shortened the cooldown on the top lane role quest's Teleport.
- **Early read on 26.18:** at the Demacia Cup (27 games, non-Worlds teams), Nocturne was banned 27 of 27 games and Cassiopeia still had 85% presence.
- **Pro vs solo queue:** in solo queue on patch 26.19 (Emerald+), the leaders are Yone (top), Wukong and Master Yi (jungle), Katarina and Ekko (mid), Jinx (bot) and Thresh (support). Cassiopeia, Orianna, Ryze, Akali, Nocturne, Vi and Jarvan IV are not in the solo-queue top 20 for their roles. Locke has a 30% solo-queue ban rate. — [lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/tierlist/?lane=middle) — Oct 2026
