## Checked beliefs
- **CHANGED:** "The meta shifts every patch." Patches still ship every two weeks, but the top picks in each role barely moved from 26.18 to 26.19. Real shifts build up over several patches, usually from outliers and item changes:
  - Senna bot: 18.8% pick rate and 56.6% win rate on 26.12, down to 0.4% pick rate by 26.18.
  - Yunara: 4.7% pick rate on 26.12, 16.1% on 26.19. She rose after Runaan's Hurricane was buffed in 26.16, which is the likely but unproven cause.
  - Late-season patches (26.18 to 26.20) are mostly tuned for pro play ahead of Worlds.
  - Sources: [lolalytics](https://lolalytics.com/lol/tierlist/) (Emerald+, its API for patches 16.12 to 16.19), [Riot patch schedule](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/gameplay/patch-schedule-league-of-legends/). As of 26.19.
- **MOSTLY CONFIRMED:** the staples list.
  - Lee Sin is the most-picked jungler (12.9%) but wins slightly below average.
  - Thresh is the top support (15%) and strong.
  - Kai'Sa (18%), Jhin (17%) and Caitlyn (15%, 22.6% ban rate) are top-5 ADCs.
  - Yasuo, Ahri and Yone are top-5 mid; Yone is the most-picked top laner.
  - Caveats: Ezreal is still picked 11% of the time but has the worst win rate of any popular ADC (about 45.8%). Lux is a support staple mainly at low elo (13% of games across all ranks, 6.6% in Emerald+).
  - Missing from the list: Viktor (most-picked mid), Jinx (strongest ADC), Yunara (16%), Lulu (14%), Malphite/Garen/Darius/Aatrox (top), Viego/Graves/Kha'Zix (jungle).
  - Sources: [op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions?position=adc), lolalytics, [u.gg stats feed](https://stats2.u.gg/lol/1.5/champion_ranking/world/16_19/ranked_solo_5x5/emerald_plus/1.5.0.json). Patch 26.19, Sept 23 – Oct 6, 2026.
- **CONFIRMED (dates are live dates):**
  - Mel: Jan 23, 2025 (patch 25.S1.2). [25.S1.2 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-25-s1-2-notes/)

  - Zaahen: "the Unsundered", a Darkin fighter, Nov 19, 2025 (25.23). [25.23 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-25-23-notes/)
- **CHANGED:** "about 172 champions" → **173**, with Locke. Data Dragon 16.19.1 returns 173. [Data Dragon](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.19.1/data/en_US/champion.json), Sept 2026.

Meddler (Riot): "the Season 2 champ will be the only champ this year, with most of the team focused on League Next" (the 2027 overhaul). Riot also promised two reworks for 2026. [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/lol/riot-games-confirms-only-one-new-champion-for-2026-amid-league-next-development/), Mar 3, 2026.

## New findings
- **Patch numbering.** Riot's notes call it 26.x, but the client and stats sites say 16.x. 2025 ran 25.S1.1–25.S1.3, then 25.04–25.24.
- **Current patch.** 26.19 went live Sept 23, 2026. 26.20, the Worlds patch, is due Oct 7. [26.19 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-19-notes/)
- **Most-picked and strongest per role** (26.19, Emerald+). Pick rates agree across the three sites to within about 0.5 points. Win rates below are op.gg/u.gg figures, where the baseline is about 50%.
  - **Top.** Most picked: Yone 8.5%, Malphite 7.1%, Aatrox, Garen, Darius (about 6.4% each). Strong: Malphite 51.5% (16.6% ban rate), Ornn, Teemo, Shen. Weak: Jayce 47.8%, K'Sante 47.4%.
  - **Jungle.** Most picked: Lee Sin, Viego 8.6%, Graves 8.5%, Kha'Zix 7.9%. Strong: Wukong 52.1%, Rammus 52.3%, Cho'Gath 51.3% (6.8% pick rate), Warwick, Lillia.
  - **Mid.** Most picked: Viktor 9.3%, Ahri 9.0%, Yasuo 9.1%, Zed 8.2%, Yone, Syndra, Locke 6.7%. Strong: Vex 52.1%, Ekko 51.5%, Fizz, Twisted Fate, Xerath. Weak: Orianna 46.3%, Ryze 45.6%, Mel 45.5%.
  - **Bot.** Most picked: Kai'Sa, Jinx 17%, Jhin, Yunara 16%, Caitlyn, Ezreal, Tristana 10.5%. Strong: Jinx 52.4%, Tristana 51.4%, Xayah, Zeri. Weak: Ezreal, Vayne 47.6%, Lucian 47.8%, Smolder 49%.
    - Mages and Yasuo in bot show high win rates at 1–2% pick rate. These are small, self-selected samples, not meta.
  - **Support.** Most picked: Thresh, Lulu, Nautilus 9.8%, Nami 9%. Strong: Thresh 52.0%, Braum 52.3%, Leona 51.9%, Blitzcrank 51.6%. Weak: Mel 42%, Karma 48.8%.
  - **Bans (Emerald+).** Locke 29.8%, Nasus 25%, Zed 24.5%, Pyke 24%, Yasuo 22.7%, Caitlyn 22.6%. In Master+, Pyke is at 35.7% and Locke at 34%.
  - **Korea, Master+** ([op.gg](https://op.gg/lol/champions?position=jungle&region=kr&tier=master_plus)) looks more like pro play:
    - Lee Sin: 24.7% pick rate, 56% ban rate.
    - Yunara: 26% pick rate.
    - Jayce (top): 10.5% pick rate.
    - Viktor: 12% pick rate.
    - Lulu: 18% pick rate.
- **What Riot thinks is mis-tuned.**
  - 26.20 preview (Sept 29):
    - Nerfs:
      - Ambessa, Cassiopeia and K'Sante, described as "stale in pro play".
      - Ashe, part of "one of the dominant bot-lane pairings".
      - Yunara, for her level-1 laning.
      - Experimental Hexplate on ranged champions (top-lane Vayne).
      - Runaan's Hurricane, whose 26.16 buff "went too far".
    - Buffs: Lucian, Vayne, Smolder, Neeko, Lillia, Diana (jungle) and others.
    - Varus and Sylas changes were pulled.
    - [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-20-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/)
  - K'Sante and Cassiopeia already win under 50% in solo queue, so pro and solo balance are pulling in different directions.
  - 26.19 preview, from Phroxzon (Riot's lead gameplay designer):
    - Top lane is strong at low and mid elo but loses influence at high elo.
    - Lucian mid and top win "high-30s to low-40s".
    - Orianna was spared a nerf because she is weak in ranked.
    - [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/lol-patch-26-19-preview-all-buffs-nerfs-and-more/), Sept 15.
- **Builds and keystones** (op.gg, Emerald+, 26.19).
  - **Crit ADCs** run Lethal Tempo almost universally: Yunara and Jinx 98%, Kai'Sa 94%, Caitlyn 82%. Jhin runs Fleet Footwork (70%).
  - **Hexoptics C44** is a new crit item from 26.1:
    - Jinx's most common core is Hexoptics → Runaan's → Infinity Edge (43% of her games).
    - Caitlyn's core is Hexoptics → Infinity Edge → Rapid Firecannon or Lord Dominik's.
  - **Kai'Sa** goes Kraken Slayer → Guinsoo's → Terminus.
  - **Deathfire Touch** is a new Sorcery keystone from 26.9 and now dominates mages: Viktor 93%, Smolder 90%, Brand 78%, Malzahar 69%, Cassiopeia 68%.
  - **Stormraider's Surge** (also new in 26.9): Hecarim 86%.
  - **Phase Rush was removed in 26.9.** [26.9 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-9-notes/)
  - **Conqueror** on Lee Sin (91%) and Aatrox (89%), building Eclipse → Black Cleaver or Sundered Sky → Death's Dance.
  - **Electrocute** on Ahri (87%), Aurora (95%) and Locke (91%). Locke builds Lich Bane → Shadowflame → Zhonya's.
  - **Engage supports** take Aftershock and build Locket → Knight's Vow → Bandlepipes (new item). Enchanters take Aery.

- **Locke's trajectory** (lolalytics, Emerald+).
  - Launch: 73% ban rate on 26.13–26.14, and a 54.0% win rate on 26.14 against a bracket average of about 51.8%.
  - He was nerfed in 26.15, and his ban rate has fallen to about 30%.
  - His win rate is now about average in Emerald+, above average in Master+ and 48.9% across all ranks.
- **Mel** still wins only 45–47% after the 26.3 rework, yet she is banned in 12–20% of games.
- **Context the prior lacks** ([26.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/), Jan 8, 2026):
  - Role quests with per-role rewards (for example level 20 and Unleashed Teleport for top).
  - A batch of new items.
  - Base critical damage set to 200%.
  - Atakhan and Feats of Strength removed.
  - Minions now spawn at 0:30 and Baron at 20:00.
  - Later patches: 26.16 raised ADC magic resist across the board and buffed tank/AP jungle pets ([26.16 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-16-notes/)).
