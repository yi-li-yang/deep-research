## Wrong claims and corrections

1. **Mizuno Seisuke is "about 70"** (#5). He was born on 24 Jan 1950 (昭和25年), so he is 76 as of Oct 2026. The guild page's "齢70" was written around 2020.
2. **A recent Tsunesaburō HAP40 "Miki" plane with a flat back** (#17). The 酒壺 (Miki) HAP40 line is not new; it was on sale by 2020. It comes in two versions, ウラ出し不要 and ウラ出し必要. Tsunesaburō describes the no-ura-dashi blade as having no side lands and a large radius (Ｒ) from the kaisaki to the edge. It is not a flat back.
3. **Tasai has a 1–2 year wait** (#21). A US dealer reports the order book is over three years long and closed to new orders, and has been closed since at least 2025 (Sunday Carpenter). Mandaraya (2024) says production cannot keep up.
4. **Kanayama Tetsuo as evidence of Tokyo's decline** (#26). His 2024 closure is real, but he moved the family workshop from Tokyo to Toyama after taking it over in 1955. He was a Toyama smith, so his closure says nothing about Tokyo.
5. **Japan Post advises against B2C parcels under €150 to AT, DK, DE, FI, FR, BE, PT and LU** (#66). This is out of date, and it was a ban rather than advice. Japan Post's 13 Jul 2026 notice barred IOSS (B2C) mail to those 8 countries from 24 Jul. Its 1 Oct 2026 notice replaced that with country-by-country rules:
   - IOSS mail cannot be sent to Austria, Czechia, Germany, Belgium, Portugal or Réunion.
   - Denmark, Finland, France and Luxembourg (among others) accept it only with duties prepaid (DDP) through Zonos.
   - Several other states accept it only as DAP.
   - Non-IOSS mail is accepted everywhere.
6. **Tsunesaburō's HSS and HAP40 blades have a flat back and need no ura-dashi** (#122). Only HSS is described as not needing ura-dashi. HAP (酒壺) is sold in both ura-dashi-required and ura-dashi-free versions, and the ura-dashi-free version has a radiused back, not a flat one.
7. **The casino domain is "tools-from-japan.com"** (#129). That hostname does not resolve. The store's actual domain, toolsfromjapan.com, now returns a 301 redirect to lourand.com, a crypto-casino affiliate page.
